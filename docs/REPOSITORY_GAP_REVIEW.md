## Main assessment

Alvorada is a substantial, traceable research framework with unusually explicit
authority and evidence boundaries. Its principal weakness is that **breadth of
documentation exceeds demonstrated operational feasibility and decision value**.
The recent latency, adoption and record-value additions specify investigations;
they do not resolve the underlying empirical questions.

The next step should be to consolidate one usable path and obtain independent
human evidence, rather than add more leaders, sectors or principles. Public
research publication can continue with clear limitations. Institutional reliance,
claims of superiority and full constitutional adoption require different evidence.

## Priorities and interpretation

**P1:** address before higher institutional reliance or a claim requiring the
missing evidence. **P2:** next repository/research improvement. **P3:** later
expansion or distribution improvement. Priority does not imply an existing
production incident or that public research must stop.

Findings distinguish missing evidence from incomplete implementation and
normative questions. A proposal already acknowledging a gap is still unfinished,
but acknowledgement is a strength. The completion criteria below are proposed
evidence requirements, not automatic adoption or deployment gates.

Do not supply invented staffing, grants, privacy permissions, reviewers or results
to make this sequence appear complete. Tasks can proceed in parallel where
independent; execution depends on actual prerequisites, not elapsed time.

## What should be preserved

- Original decisions, accepted requirements, proposed constitutional text and
  frozen observations remain traceable and unchanged.
- Separation of recommendation, authority, execution and verification remains
  useful even if record formats are simplified.
- Narrower records retain material reasons, uncertainty, dissent and history;
  simplification does not delete mandatory evidence.
- Competent conventional comparators, legitimate countercases and adverse
  findings remain necessary to assess added value.
- An implementation gap is not proof of constitutional contradiction; a recorded
  zero-gap review is not proof that every future critique is resolved.
