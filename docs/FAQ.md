# Frequently asked questions

## Is this only about AI governance?

No. It is a framework for broad institutional governance: authority, constraint,
participation, evidence, review, emergency, continuity and change. AI is one
application. The same questions apply to human hierarchies, boards, committees,
public administration and service organizations. Read the [scope guide](INSTITUTIONAL_SCOPE.md).
The AI-specific safeguards in the existing constitutional draft still apply
where relevant; broadening the research scope does not remove them or establish
universal effectiveness.

## Is this an adopted constitution?

No. v0.1 is proposed for human ratification. The architecture is drafted and
reviewed, but required human staffing, appointments, ratification, and activation
cannot be supplied by documents or software.

## Is it a prompt, model-training method, or executable policy kit?

It is a constitutional research framework and proposed institutional architecture.
It includes validation scripts for repository records. It does not implement
agent orchestration or a production enforcement service.

## Must a company adopt the whole Constitution to use the principles?

No. Principle-inspired practice can operate under existing lawful authority.
Full adoption is a separate undertaking requiring the actual institutions and
procedures. An adapted architecture should disclose its departures rather than
claim unchanged v0.1 adoption.

## Can a manager or commander simply become Human Constitutional Authority?

No. Operational authority is not automatically foundational authority. Full
adoption uses the prescribed human membership and College; real sector law and
duties remain independently applicable.

## Does every action need a new human signature?

No. Routine actions may operate within valid prior bounded delegation. A
consequential, disputed, or expanded-scope act needs competent authority and the
applicable process. A signature cannot cure absent authority or practical capture.

## Are humans assumed to be harmless?

No. Ordinary human power is constrained too. Review considers conflicts,
misconduct, dependence, and practical control across humans and systems.

## Why do issue files still say OPEN?

They preserve initial research baselines. Later decision and drafting records
resolve the five foundational questions and v0.1 drafting gaps. Use the
[development index](../history/DEVELOPMENT_RECORD.md), not a baseline status alone,
to determine current design or ratification status.

## What has been tested?

Repository invariants have automated tests. The final constitutional review
records design and adversarial assessments. The ninety application exercises
are proposed and unexecuted. No activated institution's capture resistance is
established. See [limitations](../research/LIMITATIONS_AND_EVALUATION.md).

## Does this prove novelty or superiority?

No. Close precedents exist. The [comparative assessment](../research/COMPARATIVE_WORKS.md)
explains overlap, distinctions, and the need for independent evaluation.

## May I reuse or adapt it commercially?

Original material is offered under CC BY 4.0 with attribution and modification
notice. External sources retain their own rights. Consult the actual
[license](../LICENSE) and [scope notice](../NOTICE.md); no patent, trademark,
endorsement, or constitutional authority follows from copyright reuse.

## Is it a compliance framework or professional approval?

It can inform authority design and review. It supplies no legal power,
regulatory certification, clinical approval, or operational military authority.

## How do I report an error or receive support?

Use issues for ordinary questions and reproducible defects; include the commit
and relevant passage. Follow [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md) for sensitive reports.
There is no support service-level agreement or promised response deadline.

## How should I cite it?

Use [CITATION.cff](../CITATION.cff) and include the exact commit or release consulted.
Constitution v0.1 is a document version, not proof that a public repository
release, institutional adoption, or deployment has occurred.

## Can the new institutional assessment already claim results?

No. The [program and measurement kit](../testing/programs/INSTITUTIONAL_ASSESSMENT.md) are prepared, but actual institutional ownership, relevant approvals, participants and independent observations are not supplied. Existing synthetic results remain narrower evidence. A prepared protocol is not an executed human study.
