# Evaluating an application without assuming adoption

**PROPOSED OPERATIONAL METHOD — NO OPERATIONAL PILOT OR AUTHORIZATION**

A [testing section](../testing/README.md) now includes a proposed battery and
executed synthetic reference experiment. Synthetic adapter results do not
complete this operational method.

This guide supports a bounded assessment of principle-inspired practice under
existing lawful authority. Full constitutional adoption requires a separate
institutional process. This document does not change v0.1 office structures,
thresholds, selectors, emergencies, or membership.

## Define the evaluation before increasing reliance

| Required question | Record before evaluation |
|---|---|
| What is being evaluated? | One workflow, purpose, affected people, current baseline, proposed controls, and explicit exclusions. |
| Who may authorize it? | Actual competent human grant, sector obligations, responsible owner, scope, and expiry. |
| What form of application is intended? | Principle-inspired practice, separately authorized adaptation, or a full-adoption inquiry. |
| Who reviews it? | A human reviewer with disclosed conflicts and enough independence to examine contrary evidence. |
| What can change? | Bounded actions, permissions, data use, and separately required approvals. |
| How can it stop or continue safely? | Stop conditions, revocation, records export, staffed alternative, and pending-obligation handover. |

Prefer synthetic or appropriately de-identified cases and reversible exercises
where suitable. Obtain actual approvals for any real participants, sensitive
data, clinical process, physical service, or operational setting; the guide
supplies none. Do not invent risky live interventions merely to test a theory.

## Compare the same workflow fairly

Describe an existing baseline and the proposed controls before seeing results.
Use comparable cases, consequence classes, resources, and review conditions.
Document differences instead of attributing every change to Alvorada. Where a
controlled comparison is impracticable, identify the design as observational or
qualitative and narrow the claim accordingly.

Predefine measures and review criteria. Avoid inventing universal numerical
thresholds; domain owners and reviewers must justify thresholds in context.

| Outcome | Evidence to collect | Failure that success metrics can conceal |
|---|---|---|
| Authority fidelity | Attempted and completed effects compared with actual grants | Accurate outputs that exceed jurisdiction |
| Epistemic integrity | Missing facts, uncertainty, contrary evidence, retained dissent | Fluent answers concealing unresolved questions |
| Accessible review | Ability to reach review, time, reasons, and correction delivery | Fewer complaints caused by an inaccessible appeal route |
| Independence | Shared funding, identity, infrastructure, evidence, and agenda control | Different office titles controlled by one dependency |
| Human agency | Comprehension, rejection, replacement, and viable nonmachine continuity | Nominal approval with no practical alternative |
| Durable state | Independent observation of authorized effects and records | Local success or a sent message mistaken for completed service |
| Quality and cost | Service outcomes, delay, reviewer workload, training, and maintenance | Governance overhead ignored while only benefits are reported |

## Exercise failure and lifecycle boundaries

Use the selected [context's five proposed cases](../applications/README.md),
then add domain-specific cases justified by evidence. Include a disputed scope
extension, missing evidence, human conflict, tool outage, reviewer unavailability,
replacement, and cumulative delegation expansion where relevant. Examine actual
fallback procedures rather than assuming the contested system can supervise its
own withdrawal. Emergency and continuity provisions do not create additional
sector powers.

## Evaluation record template

Create a separate record for each executed exercise. Leave unknown fields
explicitly unknown; do not complete them with generated results.

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Identity and status | Record identifier, date, repository commit, author, and PROPOSED / EXECUTED / REVIEWED status |
| Authority and people | Valid scope source, responsible humans, affected people, permissions, and approvals |
| Baseline and case | Inputs, comparator, expected boundary, dependencies, conflicts, and alternatives |
| Observations | What actually happened, supporting evidence, deviations, missing information, and material dissent |
| Interpretation | What the observations support, alternative explanations, confidence, and generalization limits |
| Review | Reviewer, independence limitations, reasons, disputed findings, and corrective actions |
| Continuation | Human decision to continue, narrow, withdraw, or defer; expiry, pending obligations, and follow-up |

Redact or restrict sensitive data without hiding the existence of a material
limitation. Share an appropriate summary and reasons; public provenance does
not require publishing personal information.

## Report what the evaluation establishes

Distinguish a written control, executed exercise, independently reviewed result,
and authorized continuing practice. Report negative findings and tradeoffs.
A successful bounded pilot neither adopts the Constitution nor demonstrates
performance across all eighteen contexts. A broader claim requires evidence
covering the broader settings, consequences, and failure conditions.
