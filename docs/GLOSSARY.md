# Glossary

**Artificial intelligence:** Computational systems that perform activities
associated with cognition, analysis, generation, prediction, or action.

**Authority:** A recognized permission or competence to make a consequential
institutional decision or act.

**Capability:** Practical ability to perform an action. Capability alone does
not establish authority.

**Constitutional Decision Record (CDR):** A traceable record of a constitutional
question, alternatives, reviews, dissent, and an explicit human decision when
one is made.

**Constitutional provision:** A rule in human-readable constitutional text.
Proposed provisions exist in Constitution v0.1; none is operative merely by
publication, and `constitution/` contains no adopted provisions.

**Effective power:** Practical ability to shape institutional outcomes,
including power not reflected in formal grants.

**Formal authority:** Authority represented by applicable institutional rules.

**Human Constitutional Authority (HCA):** Human foundational constitutional
authority. Proposed v0.1 specifies a membership-rooted seven-human College and
its lifecycle; the relevant human ratification and activation conditions remain
unsatisfied. A repository maintainer or ordinary manager is not automatically HCA.

**Institutional function:** A generic activity such as proposing, reviewing,
adjudicating, authorizing, executing, certifying, auditing, or recording. A
function is not automatically a constitutional office.

**Provenance:** Traceable information about the source, lineage, review, and
authorization of a record or proposition.

**Refounding:** Explicit human replacement of foundational institutional
identity, distinct from ordinary or structural amendment. Proposed v0.1
protects ten commitments at this level in VIII.1 and requires acknowledgment
of discontinuity. The boundary is specified in the proposal.

**Substrate:** The biological or computational basis of an actor exercising a
capability.

**YAML Ain't Markup Language (YAML):** A human-readable structured data format.
The bootstrap records use its JavaScript Object Notation-compatible subset.
