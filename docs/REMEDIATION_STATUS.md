## New design questions introduced by this remediation

The common charter and profiles need their own normative review, traceability and
specific adoption instrument before being described as a complete new Constitution.
Exact tenure, selectors, renewal/removal, constituencies, independence standards,
proof burdens, confidentiality and change processes must be filled, not left as
ranges or UNKNOWN. The original 35 requirements were reviewed against v0.1;
that status does not transfer to a materially different charter/profile.

Proportional classification can miss severe reliance or cumulative harm. AI
support can move costs into verification, entrench sponsor control or suppress
minority evidence. Recursion can launder AI ratings into apparent success. These
are explicitly addressed design risks and still require observation.
