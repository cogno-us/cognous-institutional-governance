## Constitutional Architecture

1. **Identity and human sovereignty:** Foundational institutional ends remain
   under legitimate Human Constitutional Authority, exercised after activation
   through a membership-rooted seven-human College.
2. **Authority and separation:** Human and artificial power is constitutionally
   constrained; authorization, adjudication, certification, verification,
   enforcement, succession, continuity, and transition functions remain
   separated.
3. **Adjudication and remedies:** A nine-human Constitutional Court exercises
   bounded review. It may decide legality and declare vacancies but cannot
   appoint, amend, refound, or control institutional ends.
4. **Records and effective power:** Constitutional acts require attributable
   provenance, reasons, dissent, dependency disclosure, durable history, and
   review of formal and effective power.
5. **Emergency authority:** Emergency action requires prior delegation or a
   narrowly bounded human necessity process and cannot become succession,
   amendment, refounding, or foundational authority.
6. **Succession and interregnum:** Membership-predesignated successors,
   independently reviewed proof, claimant-neutral continuity, bounded acting
   service, hard time limits, and human fallbacks prevent succession-created
   sovereignty.
7. **Constitutional change:** Ordinary amendment, structural amendment, and
   explicit refounding remain distinct, with aggregation and semantic-drift
   safeguards against amendment laundering.
8. **Refounding, termination, and memory:** Refounding requires explicit human
   acknowledgment of discontinuity; termination and transition remain bounded,
   accountable, obligation-preserving, and historically non-erasing.

- constitutional membership and standing channels not controlled by machines;
- human sovereignty over foundational ends subject to constitutional
  constraint in every exercise of institutional power;
- a seven-human, directly membership-elected Human Constitutional Authority
  College with independent certification, cause-bounded removal, constituent
  confirmation, no holdover, and separately recognized predesignated
  successors;
- bounded human adjudication with recusal, conflict tribunals, appeal, and
  nonappointive remedies;
- separately selected Verification and Enforcement Offices with separated
  removal and replacement chains;
- a closed, structurally protected Schedule O selector class;
- prelimited emergency, succession, continuity, transition, amendment,
  refounding, and termination authority;

  competence, performance, reliance, emergency, adjudication, succession,

and may assist administration, evidence handling, analysis, provenance, and
formal validation. It may not vote, hold foundational authority, select,
appoint, remove, or replace constitutional officers, control eligibility or
sortition, certify human constitutional legitimacy, recognize successors, or
exercise final constitutional judgment.

## Effect of Ratification

A valid ratification decision would adopt the proposed text as Alvorada's
Constitution through attributable Human Constitutional Authority. The
ratification instrument must identify the exact proposed artifact and supply
the initial Schedule O required by Section III.2, containing at least three
separately held, materially independent qualifying human offices.

Ratification would authorize only the constitutional order and the bounded
initialization expressly stated in the Constitution. It would not itself:

- deploy or approve a production governance runtime;
- approve software, identity, voting, storage, evidence, or enforcement
  systems;
- deem subordinate governance law enacted;
- waive testing, security, accessibility, audit, or implementation review;
- appoint an artificial-intelligence system or grant one foundational
  authority; or
- convert analysis, validation, capability, reliance, necessity, or effective
  control into constitutional authority.
