## Question

Does constitutional exclusion of AI from membership imply absence of moral
status or protection, or should future doctrine explicitly separate protected
interests, legal personality, constitutional membership, delegated agency, and
foundational sovereignty?

No conclusion about AI consciousness, subjective experience, moral status, or
legal personality is established by this record. Fictional examples supply
questions, not evidence. Public Substack material has not been independently
reviewed for this work package.

## Current constitutional boundary

II.2(2) excludes AI from constitutional membership. I.1(22–25) denies machine
foundational sovereignty and capability-based authority. VII.1(10) and
VIII.1(4) protect the human membership root and human-only foundational
allocations at the refounding level. Research cannot alter those provisions.

Moral status, protections, legal personality, membership, agency, and sovereignty
are distinct questions; resolving one does not automatically resolve the others.
A possible protected interest need not imply political or foundational authority.
Human constitutional status is not conditional on outperforming machines.

- Classify any eventual proposal under the existing change procedures. A change
