## Executive Determination

The draft now contains a complete human, membership-rooted lifecycle for the
seven-member Human Constitutional Authority College:

1. the attributable ratification instrument opens seven numbered-seat
   elections decided directly by fixed constitutional membership;
2. an independent temporary human panel certifies process and result without
   selecting winners or creating legitimacy;
3. recurring elections begin automatically, failed elections cannot create
   holdover, and exhausted alternate slates invoke special membership
   elections;
4. removal requires narrow cause, independent human fact-finding,
   nonconflicted constituent confirmation, and bounded nonappointive review;
5. death, permanent loss, resignation, temporary incapacity, and final removal
   are the exclusive lifecycle triggers;
6. temporary incapacity permits only bounded acting service, while final
   removal creates a permanent vacancy only after review finality; and
7. recognition remains limited to membership-predesignated successors through
   the adopted Succession Council or fallback panel.

No Court, reviewer, office, machine, incumbent, emergency actor, or effective
controller may appoint a College member, create an unlisted succession event,
extend a term, manufacture a successor, or acquire foundational authority.

The initial Schedule O population remains a mandatory component of the later
ratification instrument. It is a required ratification input, not an unresolved
constitutional design decision or authority for subordinate law to classify
offices.
