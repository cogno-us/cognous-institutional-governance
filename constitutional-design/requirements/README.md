# Constitutional requirements

All 35 requirements below are accepted **for drafting**, derived from the five
explicit human foundational decisions. They constrain proposed v0.1; they are
not independently operative constitutional law.

| Source decision | Requirements | Subject |
|---|---|---|
| [CDR-001](../decisions/CDR-001.yaml) | CR-001–CR-006 | Human sovereignty and binding constraint |
| [CDR-002](../decisions/CDR-002.yaml) | CR-007–CR-013 | Emergency authority |
| [CDR-003](../decisions/CDR-003.yaml) | CR-014–CR-020 | Continuity and succession |
| [CDR-004](../decisions/CDR-004.yaml) | CR-021–CR-029 | Adjudication and review |
| [CDR-005](../decisions/CDR-005.yaml) | CR-030–CR-035 | Amendment and refounding |

The [consolidated map](../CONSOLIDATED_REQUIREMENT_MAP.yaml) maps these into
20 drafting groups. The [traceability record](../drafts/CONSTITUTION_v0.1_TRACEABILITY.yaml)
shows their incorporation into the proposed Constitution's 24 sections.

Use [REQUIREMENT_TEMPLATE.yaml](REQUIREMENT_TEMPLATE.yaml) for new records.
Allowed statuses are `CANDIDATE`, `ACCEPTED_FOR_DRAFTING`, `DISPUTED`, `REJECTED`,
and `SUPERSEDED`. Acceptance requires source decision provenance and, where
required, explicit human authorization. A requirement is not an implementation
standard, deployment grant, appointment, or ratification act.
