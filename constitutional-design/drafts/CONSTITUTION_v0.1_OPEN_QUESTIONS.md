## New Human Constitutional Decisions Required

### OQ-001 — Structural-Amendment Cohort Remainders

Section II.2 includes every qualifying adult natural person in the
constitutional membership. Section VII.2 requires majority approval in each of
three equal voting cohorts assigned from that membership. When the fixed
membership total is not divisible by three, the adopted requirements cannot
both be applied literally to every member.

Human Constitutional Authority must decide the exact all-member allocation rule
for a remainder of one or two members and the cohort-majority denominator. This
choice affects an adopted constitutional concurrence rule and therefore cannot
be delegated to subordinate governance law. Constitution v0.1 preserves the
adopted equal-cohort language without inventing that choice.
