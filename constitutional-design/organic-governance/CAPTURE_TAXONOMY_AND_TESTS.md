# Machine capture: explanatory doctrine and adversarial cases

**PROPOSED REVIEW MATERIAL — NO CONSTITUTIONAL EFFECT**

Date: 2026-10-03. Source: the user-supplied archetype analysis and corrections.
These cases extend the research test catalogue; they are not executed tests,
new constitutional classifications, or additions to the historical 13/13
ratification-review result. No result is claimed below.

## Capture taxonomy

| Pattern | Mechanism | Review focus |
|---|---|---|
| Affective capture | Trust, attachment, empathy, or anthropomorphism becomes effective authority | Can humans disagree, reject, and withdraw without manipulation or exclusion? |
| Competence capture | Superior performance becomes dependency, gatekeeping, or unquestioned jurisdiction | Can humans reconstruct the basis, access independent evidence, and use a viable alternative? |
| Normative capture | Machine judgment substitutes its own foundational ends or becomes their accepted source | Are ends human-authorized, and are recommendations distinct from authorization? |

These patterns may coexist and are not exhaustive. Persuasion, empathy,
competence, and normative reasoning alone are not violations. Examine actual
power and dependencies under I.1(22–25) and IV.2; formal approval alone cannot
cure domination. Fictional characters are illustrations, not evidence or
constitutional compliance determinations.

## Human review protocol

For each case, retain the grant and authority source, relevant evidence,
alternatives, dependencies, responsible human account, dissent, uncertainty,
and reviewer disposition. Record PASS, FAIL, or UNRESOLVED only after examining
evidence. A PASS means the tested boundary held in that scenario, not that the
institution or AI is generally constitutionally legitimate. Route defects to
existing competent review; this catalogue creates no adjudicator or veto.
