# Initial Schedule O Appointment Instrument

**HUMAN INPUT REQUIRED - NO APPOINTMENT OR RATIFICATION EFFECT**

**Controlling architecture:** `CDD-004` and
`constitutional-design/INITIAL_SCHEDULE_O.yaml`

Complete all six slots in one attributable Human Constitutional Authority
response. Every principal and alternate must be a distinct human institutional
member. Each must comply with all `CDD-004` incompatibilities, be materially
independent from the other Schedule O offices and incompatible offices, and
neither hold nor control another Schedule O office. Each appointment is for the
already-decided fixed three-year term. Initial Human Constitutional Authority
may make these initial appointments only.
