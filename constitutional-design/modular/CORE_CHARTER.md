# Shared core charter — proposed modular text

**PROPOSED TEXT FOR CONTEXTUAL ADAPTATION; NOT ADOPTED LAW.**

These clauses state the common constitutional layer. The profile and actual
source of authority determine its institutional effect. They preserve the
purpose of v0.1 without claiming to reproduce its detailed offices or membership.

## C1. Human purpose and bounded authority

Humans, through the competent legitimate process, determine institutional ends
and protected obligations. Human and artificial actors exercise only expressly
granted powers. Competence, popularity, reliance, necessity and successful
performance do not create authority. AI cannot acquire foundational sovereignty.

## C2. Affected people and protected agency

The institution identifies its actual governing constituency and separately the
people affected by its power. Its purposes and decisions remain subject to
applicable rights and obligations. People have an accessible route to notice,
challenge, correction and representation where needed. Automated exclusion is
not the final gatekeeper. Participation, withdrawal and protection against
retaliation are specified; a promise cannot replace actual protective capacity.

## C3. Proportional decision procedures

Governance scales with plausible consequences, reversibility, uncertainty,
exposure, reliance, vulnerability and concentration of effective power. Routine
acts may execute within a valid standing grant without individual human approval.
Materially adverse, safety-critical, foundational or genuinely disputed decisions
receive the competent human review required by the profile and controlling rules.
An unlawful act is prohibited, not merely placed in a higher review tier.

## C4. Evidence and institutional memory

Material decisions retain attributable authorization, material sources and
reasons, alternatives, uncertainty, dissent, scope, dependencies, review route
and correction history. Routine records may reference versioned standing rules.
Evidence sufficiency is claim-specific; model confidence is not a burden of proof.
Sensitive evidence has controlled access and context-specific lawful retention;
public reasons should remain useful without unnecessary personal disclosure.
Corrections do not falsify the history. Conflicts between disclosure, retention
and other obligations receive explicit competent review.

## C5. Separation and practical independence

Authorization, contested review, execution and effect verification are distinct
functions. One team may prepare or administer work across functions when its
access and conflicts permit, but no actor finally resolves its own contested
case or independently verifies its own disputed effect. Material common control,
funding, evidence and model dependencies are disclosed. Critical independence
requires practical capacity to disagree and act, not different labels or models.

## C6. Meaningful human control and AI support

AI may retrieve, summarize, translate, compare, draft, simulate and monitor, and
may execute bounded ordinary actions under real prior delegation. Humans retain
foundational ends, constitutional judgment and reserved consequential choices.
Responsible humans can inspect sources, understand material reasons, reject the
recommendation, use a workable alternative and control timing and effect.
Neither human signatures nor model agreement establish those conditions.

## C7. Contestation, enforcement and remedy

The profile identifies accessible intake, nonconflicted review, relevant appeal,
actual execution responsibility and independent evidence of completed remedy.
Pending or refused delivery remains visible. The institution identifies which
remedies it can supply and which require external authority. Funding, replacement
and withdrawal arrangements cannot silently eliminate protected challenges.

## C8. Emergency, succession and continuity

Urgent actions use pre-existing bounded incident grants where possible. An
exceptional route needs explicit human authorization rules, triggers, evidence,
limits, expiry and independent review. Delay and action harms are both considered;
urgency cannot legitimate prohibited acts. Turnover, outages and institutional
termination preserve pending claims and duties without enlarging powers.

## C9. Authorized change and recursive improvement

Observation and critique can generate proposed improvements, including to
review procedures themselves. Changes identify sponsor, affected people,
evidence, alternatives, dissent, classification, competent approval, effective
date and recovery route. Material structural or foundational changes use the
profile's expressly reserved human process. Cumulative drift is reviewed.
No AI, reviewer or incumbent acquires amendment power through successful use.

## What must still be settled locally

The charter deliberately does not supply legal jurisdiction, legitimate
constituencies, staffing, numerical risk thresholds, rights balances or empirical
proof. Complete the [profile schedule](../../process/templates/INSTITUTIONAL_PROFILE.md)
and the [rights/records/interface procedure](../../process/RIGHTS_RECORDS_AND_INTERFACES.md).
A signed charter with missing capacity is not demonstrated institutional adoption.
