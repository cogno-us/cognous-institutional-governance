# Institutional structure, tenure and continuity

**DESIGN GUIDANCE; numerical examples are proposals, not proven optima.**

Tenure should protect judgment, continuity and competence while keeping power
contestable. Nine-year nonrenewable judicial terms may support a constitutional
reference design but are a poor default for a commercial service review role.
Short terms also carry risks: sponsor leverage at renewal, frequent retraining,
loss of memory and difficulty recruiting independent reviewers.

## Separate office tenure from other clocks

An employment contract, governance appointment, delegation expiry, case response
deadline, evidence-retention period and policy review date are different things.
A quarterly review does not end every employee's authority. A two-year appointment
does not justify retaining all personal evidence for two years. A short deadline
does not lower a controlling proof standard.

| Profile | Illustrative appointment design | Continuity and independence safeguards |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial/service | Accountable sponsor within actual board/executive mandate; independent review appointment for 12 months, renewable once through a nonconflicted selector | Quarterly dependency/resource review; separately authorized successor before expiry; cause-based removal with independent challenge; paid external alternate |
| Public administration | Use statutory offices and terms first; an additional advisory appointment might be 2–4 years if lawful and expressly authorized | Do not replace elected mandates, statutory tribunals or legally required appeals; disclose political/funding control |
| Safety-critical/coercive | Competent professional or command offices retain controlling lawful tenure; supplemental assurance appointment might be 1–3 years | Competency and conflict review before work; secure evidence access, independent escalation and continuity coverage |
| Community/small institution | Volunteer review appointment of 6–12 months, renewable once through the disclosed human process | Shared external reviewer for conflicts; handover of pending cases; rotation cannot erase obligations |

These ranges are design starting points. Choose exact values in a completed
schedule; do not leave a range as the operative term. Early-stage institutions
may lack qualified independent pools and should narrow the intended activity
rather than claim a miniature constitutional Court exists.

## A minimum functional arrangement for routine commercial work

| Function | Example allocation | Cannot claim |
|---|---|---|
| Ends and standing grant | Competent board/executive and accountable service policy owner | Universal sovereignty over customers or legal authority not actually held |
| Preparation and routine execution | Service team with optional bounded AI assistance | Power to change the policy or decide reserved adverse exceptions |
| Contested review | Noninvolved designated reviewer; external route for shared conflict | Independence merely because the reviewer has a different job title |
| Remedy delivery | Team with actual refund/correction permissions | Completion from a recommendation or a closed ticket alone |
| Effect verification | Noninvolved person checking original transaction/delivery evidence | Independent verification from the same AI's assertion |

Distinct functions do not always need five permanent committees. Preparation and
routine execution can share a team. A case's reviewer can prepare a proposed
remedy but must not certify their own disputed implementation. Higher stakes,
conflicts or controlling law may require separate institutions and stronger
independence. This arrangement is a proposed adaptation, not v0.1 compliance.

## Complete appointments before relying on the profile

Name the selector, eligibility, conflicts, exact term, start/expiry, renewal cap,
removal cause, competent removal reviewer, alternate, vacancy process, funding,
evidence access and external challenge. Obtain actual consent and commitments.
Check concurrent eligibility: a paper list of alternates can contain the same
unavailable people. Do not declare funding dependence immaterial without a
reasoned conflict review.

Expiry must not produce hidden holdover. Plan a lawful successor or a separately
authorized temporary continuity arrangement with narrow powers and an expiry.
Preserve claims and records, route unfinished work and disclose any period when
review is unavailable. No gap automatically transfers power to AI or the sponsor.

## Adoption dependency sequence

1. Identify actual lawful source of authority and outside obligations.
2. Agree purposes, affected-person protections and reserved decisions.
3. Specify selectors, exact terms, conflicts, funding and capable alternates.
4. Confirm the review, remedy, evidence-access and appeal chain can operate.
5. Approve bounded classes of actions and their evidence rules.
6. Orient people, make notice accessible and establish a usable alternative.
7. Begin only the authorized scope; observe costs and failures before expansion.

For full v0.1, use its actual selection chains, Schedule O and bootstrap rules.
No step in this simplified sequence substitutes for them.
