# Modular constitutional framework — design proposal

**Executive summary:** Use common protections with offices, terms and procedures suited to the institution. These are separate proposed adaptations; they do not silently change the fixed original Constitution.

[Read the detailed guidance](#detailed-guidance).

## Detailed guidance

**UNADOPTED DESIGN PROPOSAL. No office, grant or empirical finding is created.**

This is the recommended general-purpose architecture for further development:
a common charter, a context-specific institutional profile, proportional
operating procedures and an optional AI assistance module. It responds to the
owner's request to make Alvorada usable beyond the fixed v0.1 institutional form.
It is not a completed v0.2 ratification packet or a claim that its parameters
have been independently reviewed.

The [v0.1 proposal](../drafts/CONSTITUTION_v0.1.md) and its decisions, requirements,
review and hashes remain intact as the reference design. Its College has
seven-year terms, Court nine-year terms and Verification/Enforcement six-year
terms. These are specific institutional choices, not universal prerequisites
for accountable governance. Shorter commercial terms are reasonable design
alternatives; their optimum is not established here.

## Assemble the architecture

| Layer | Read | What it specifies |
|---|---|---|
| Shared charter | [Core charter](CORE_CHARTER.md) | Human ends, bounded power, meaningful challenge, evidence, correction and change |
| Institutional form | [Structure and terms](STRUCTURE_AND_TERMS.md) and a profile below | Actual selectors, tenure, independence, resources, review and external obligations |
| Operating procedure | [Proportional governance](../../process/PROPORTIONAL_GOVERNANCE.md) | Decision-specific severity, evidence sufficiency and process burden |
| AI assistance | [AI supporting institutions](../../applications/ai-assisted-institutional-governance.md) | Reduce preparation and administrative work while retaining accountable decision rights |
| Institutional learning | [Virtuous recursion](../../process/VIRTUOUS_RECURSION.md) | Observe effects, challenge assumptions, improve rules through separate authorization |
| Completed local schedule | [Profile record](../../process/templates/INSTITUTIONAL_PROFILE.md) | Parameters, departures, source of authority and unresolved prerequisites |

Profiles are alternatives, not mandatory cumulative committees:

- [Commercial and service institutions](profiles/COMMERCIAL.md).
- [Public administration and democratic institutions](profiles/PUBLIC.md).
- [Safety-critical and coercive institutions](profiles/SAFETY_CRITICAL.md).
- [Community, nonprofit and small institutions](profiles/COMMUNITY.md).

A hospital may combine a commercial organizational form with safety-critical
clinical procedures. A military logistics query and a contemplated lethal act
have different decision tiers within the same institution. A customer answer
can be highly consequential when it changes rights or influences urgent care.
Institution size, decision severity and use of AI are separate dimensions.

## Three legitimate uses

1. **Principle-inspired practice:** improve an existing workflow under its own
   actual lawful authority. No constitutional adoption is asserted.
2. **Separately authorized modular adaptation:** adopt an explicit charter and
   profile through the competent local process. Record every material departure,
   constituency and outside obligation. Authority comes from that process.
3. **Full v0.1 adoption or change:** satisfy the original text. A profile cannot
   shorten its terms or substitute a manager for its Court. After adoption,
   changes must use Article VII and the competent classification, including
   refounding when protected identity changes. Before adoption, a different
   proposal needs its own human design decisions, traceability and review.

## Review of all eight Articles

| v0.1 Article | Retain in general architecture | Context-specific treatment and departure boundary |
|---|---|---|
| I: identity and sovereignty | Humans determine ends; capability does not create authority | College size, seven-year terms and electorate are reference choices. A corporate board's legal mandate does not automatically reproduce v0.1 membership legitimacy. |
| II: constraint and functions | Binding grants, no self-created power, contestable participation | Map legal owners, workers, customers and affected people distinctly; do not label a stakeholder forum the v0.1 constituency. Smaller arrangements need a separately authorized profile. |
| III: adjudication/enforcement | Contestable decisions, conflict-free review, verified remedies | Nine judges and nine-year terms are replaced only in a separate design. A functional reviewer may be external; no party reviews its own contested action. |
| IV: evidence/effective power | Material reasons, alternatives, dissent, dependencies and non-erasure | Vary record depth and sampling, not the truth of material records. Privacy/public-memory tensions require explicit treatment. |
| V: emergency | Prior delegation first; necessity does not create authority | Write contextual incident grants and response targets. Profiles do not rewrite v0.1's exceptional proof, clocks or human requirements. |
| VI: succession | Continuity does not confer sovereignty; duties survive turnover | Define vacancies, withdrawal and handover for actual offices. Corporate succession is not automatically v0.1 sovereign succession. |
| VII: change | Interpretation is not amendment; cumulative drift matters | Name local selectors, notice, thresholds and protected changes. No automatic AI amendment; v0.1 change rules remain controlling for that design. |
| VIII: termination/memory | Protect pending obligations, affected people and attributable history | Define service exit, institutional termination, custody and retention separately; lawful limits on sensitive data need explicit review. |

## Design decisions and retained costs

- **Separate principles from constitutional parameters:** keeps sovereignty and
  accountability intelligible while avoiding an inappropriate long-tenure model.
  Cost: adaptations need explicit compatibility records and cannot inherit
  v0.1's recorded review status.
- **Scale procedure to consequences:** moves routine approval to the class of
  authorized actions and concentrates case review on consequential exceptions.
  Cost: classification error, cumulative harm and sampling misses need monitoring.
- **Separate AI support from institutional authority:** enables automation of
  preparation and bounded routine work. Cost: model error, surveillance,
  dependence and rubber-stamping must be investigated, not assumed away.
- **Make improvement recursive but authorization distinct:** evidence can improve
  the workflow and its own review. Cost: changing evaluation criteria can conceal
  failure unless each revision is visible and separately approved.
