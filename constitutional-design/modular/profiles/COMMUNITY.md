# Community, nonprofit and small-institution profile

**PROPOSED ADAPTATION; capacity and legitimacy remain contextual.**

Use for associations, small research teams, charities and community services.
Choose an actual human governing process, beneficiary protections, accessible
voice and a conflict route. Donor control is not beneficiary consent. Voluntary
membership does not erase duties to people relying on essential services.

A starting appointment design is a **six-month review role renewable once**, with
a separately agreed noninvolved selector, disclosed funding and an external
alternate for shared conflicts. Exact dates, removal cause, challenge, coverage
and case handover must be completed. This is not a six-month version of the
v0.1 Court; it is a separately authorized arrangement with recorded departures.

Routine scheduling and public information may be T0/T1. Allocation of essential
support, exclusion, safeguarding concerns and publication harming an identifiable
person receive T2/T3 review according to actual effects. Changes to the group's
ends, membership rights or protected assets use its reserved legitimate process.

Avoid committees that the group cannot staff. Share external review capacity
with genuinely independent institutions where authority and confidentiality
permit. If no nonconflicted reviewer is available, narrow or defer contested
activity and protect continuity obligations; do not invent independence.

AI can prepare minutes, translate, organize alternatives and help people submit
challenges without specialist vocabulary. Keep originals, minority accounts,
non-AI access and a safe withdrawal route. Do not require sensitive personal data
or repeated elaborate submissions to obtain simple help.

Required next work: actual participant agreement and authority, support for
vulnerable beneficiaries, sustainable volunteer workload, independent conflict
coverage, lawful privacy/retention rules and observed accessibility/correction.
