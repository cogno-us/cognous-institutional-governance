# Public administration and democratic institution profile

**PROPOSED GUIDANCE; actual law and legitimate public mandates control.**

Use for benefits/services, policy formation, public finance, education and
public procurement. Map elected authority, professional administration,
statutory adjudication, courts and affected people separately. Alvorada creates
none of those offices or jurisdictions.

Routine form assistance or public-information retrieval can use T0/T1 routes.
Benefit denial, sanctions, eligibility exclusion and rights-affecting profiling
require stronger competent review and existing procedural protections; national
policy or changes to democratic authority use reserved processes. An apparently
small individual act may be T3 at population scale or for a vulnerable person.

Use statutory terms and appointment rules. Only where a separately authorized
advisory arrangement is appropriate, consider a fixed 2–4-year term, specify the
exact number, and protect against sponsor-driven renewal/removal. Advisory
rotation does not alter elected mandates or statutory tenure.

Give people intelligible reasons, accessible non-AI intake, representation and
external challenge. AI may summarize consultation and analyze distributional
consequences, but cannot convert a sentiment count into democratic consent or
hide minority submissions through clustering. Translate and simplify alongside
the original; make omissions and disputes inspectable.

Separate legitimate public transparency from unnecessary disclosure of benefits,
medical or identity evidence. Publish decision rules and aggregate patterns;
provide protected access for competent reviewers. An internal pilot's privacy
procedure cannot settle legal public-record requirements.

Required next work: qualified jurisdictional mapping, participation and language
review, privacy/public-record review, verified independent appeal access,
resourced remedies and field observations. Neither this profile nor AI analysis
establishes public legitimacy or constitutional ratification.
