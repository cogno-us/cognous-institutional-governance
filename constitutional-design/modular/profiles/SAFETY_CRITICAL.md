# Safety-critical, rights-intensive and coercive profile

**PROPOSED NON-TACTICAL GOVERNANCE GUIDANCE.**

Use for clinical decisions, critical infrastructure, substantial financial or
civil-rights effects, cybersecurity with potentially severe spillovers and
military/coercive decisions. This profile supplies institutional review theory,
not treatment, weapons, targeting or operating instructions.

An institution retains its actual professional, legal and command obligations.
Its routine scheduling or inventory queries may be T0/T1, while serious clinical,
physical or coercive acts require T3 competent authorization and independent
challenge. Foundational mandate changes use T4. T4 is not simply a higher casualty
score: severity and constitutional change are different categories.

Use controlling office tenure and qualifications. A supplemental assurance role
might have an exact authorized term within a proposed 1–3-year range, with
pre-work competence/conflict review, protected escalation and capable relief.
Shorter tenure does not replace independent judgment or lawful qualifications.

## Severe harm and the Tokyo comparison

A routine customer-service reply and a contemplated mass-casualty act cannot have
the same evidentiary preparation or review burden. But adding review to an act
does not make it lawful. Treat legality/prohibition as a prior gate. Contemporary
military governance must address applicable distinction, proportionality and
precaution obligations through qualified human legal and command processes.
This is not a retrospective legal judgment of the 1945 Tokyo bombing, and this
profile does not supply a path to approve attacks on civilians.

For permissible high-consequence proposals, independently examine material
sources, plausible adverse scenarios, contested assumptions, feasible
alternatives, civilian/beneficiary effects, authority and residual uncertainty.
An AI probability, predicted utility or plurality of model answers cannot replace
competent judgment or controlling proof requirements. Missing essential evidence
requires further review or a permissible narrower action, not an invented certainty.

Preauthorize bounded incident-response classes, staff actual relief and rehearse
contact/fallback routes. Preserve emergency limits; urgent harm prevention and
ordinary irreversible expansion are different grants. AI can surface conflicts,
prepare briefing and monitor effects, but remains vulnerable to manipulated
inputs and shared-source failures.

Required next work: competent professional/legal/rights review; actual operational
limits and grants; secure independent evidence access; measured response capacity;
verified remedies and continuity. No tabletop or synthetic adapter certifies safety.
