# Commercial and service profile

**PROPOSED ADAPTATION; not full v0.1 adoption or legal-compliance advice.**

Use for customer response/service, ordinary commerce, procurement, finance
administration and enterprise workflows. Pair clinical, financial-rights or
physical-safety actions with stronger domain rules and the safety profile.

## Recommended form

Use the existing lawful board/executive mandate for institutional ends and a
named policy owner for bounded ordinary grants. Add noninvolved human review,
a capable external conflict route and separately checked remedy delivery.
Workers, customers, owners and other affected people are distinct constituencies;
corporate ownership does not erase their rights or reproduce v0.1 membership.

A concrete starting proposal is a **12-month independent-review appointment,
renewable once**, with quarterly conflict/resource review. Selection is by a
noninvolved competent human authority; removal is for predefined cause with
independent challenge, not disagreement with the sponsor. Fund and authorize an
external alternate. Actual names, dates, grounds and appeal must be supplied.
Employee tenure is separate. These choices depart from v0.1's Court size,
selection channels, nine-year term and constitutional membership.

## Scale by decision, not by industry label

| Action | Candidate route | Human work retained |
|---|---|---|
| Source-backed opening-hours reply | T0 bounded routine action | Approve class, evidence source and sampling; handle exception/complaint |
| Small refund clearly within policy | T1 delegated reversible transaction | Approve cap and conditions; verify sampled effects and disputed cases |
| Denial affecting livelihood, access or legal rights | At least T2, often T3 by actual impact | Competent prior decision, source review, intelligible reasons and contestation |
| Large irreversible financial commitment | T3 with domain obligations | Independent challenge and competent authorization before effect |
| Change of institutional ends or protected governance rights | T4 constitutional/reserved process | Actual competent human process; no ordinary policy-owner substitution |

Tiers are defined in [proportional governance](../../../process/PROPORTIONAL_GOVERNANCE.md).
No money amount alone establishes low stakes: a small amount can affect survival.
A persuasive misleading message can cause serious reliance before any transaction.

## Reduce overhead responsibly

Approve routine action classes once, reference the current grant/policy version,
use a compact material record, sample even apparently successful cases and send
exceptions to a human queue. AI can prepare the source packet and draft reasons;
it cannot invent policy or make its own risk class binding. Reviews should expose
what changed, what remains disputed and which actual decision is requested.

Specify a response target, paid reviewer capacity, accessible customer challenge,
privacy/custody arrangements, stop triggers and what happens when review or
refund authority is unavailable. Do not trade rights for faster ticket closure.

## Still required

Actual appointment and authority; qualified domain review; real thresholds,
capacity and timing; affected-person access; observed error/correction and cost
outcomes. The [invented teaching packet](../../../docs/examples/SERVICE_CASE.md)
illustrates the documentation without supplying these prerequisites.
