## DECISION-RD-01 — Constitutional Membership, Standing, and Separated Offices

**Canonical recommendation SHA-256:** `8ae3601414b50c1f98c44eed3be348862dfa0d8f1c0ac1f27ccddec9c9eed86d`

**Alternatives tested:** Direct human constituency; representative human
constituency; plural hybrid human membership and standing.

**Recommendation:** Adopt the plural hybrid. Constitutional membership includes
every adult natural person who, immediately before an exercise's initiation
event, was either subject to the institution's claimed authority or within its
published class of ultimate human beneficiaries; artificial intelligence is
ineligible. Initiation means filing a proposal or case, publishing office-
selection notice, filing a succession trigger, opening transition nominations,
or giving public purpose-specific notice for any other constitutional exercise.
A public pre-exercise roll is rebuttable evidence, not the source of membership,
and inclusion or exclusion must be reviewable before participation. Provide four standing
channels: personal constitutional injury, representative standing with
attributable authorization, public-interest standing for material institutional
violations, and office standing within assigned functions. Separate
authorization, adjudication, enforcement, evidence validation, independent
verification, succession administration, and transition custody. No office
combines final judgment with enforcement or evidence control.

**Decision required:** Authority-or-beneficiary membership test; exhaustive
initiation-event rule and reviewable pre-exercise roll; four enumerated standing
channels; seven-function separation; limits on combined offices.

## DECISION-RD-02 — Adjudicator Composition and Independence

**Canonical recommendation SHA-256:** `3e9b721a4021a2e6b83f747eb5394eea183b766b4c928f09ab78f608c9e020eb`

**Alternatives tested:** Single-channel standing tribunal; distributed
adjudicative bodies; hybrid standing court with distributed appointments and
special conflict handling.

**Recommendation:** Establish a nine-human-member Constitutional Court. Fixed
constitutional membership elects three seats with participation above one half:
each member may approve three eligible candidates, the three highest totals
win, and final-seat ties use public human sortition. All nonconflicted ordinary
human officeholders elect three seats with two-thirds participation under the
same rules. Public human sortition fills three seats from a qualified pool.
That pool contains volunteering constitutional members who disclose conflicts
and material dependencies, complete a public human-administered constitutional
competence review by the Verification Office with one Court appeal, and for
three years held no governance, enforcement, institutional artificial-
intelligence operation, or material contracting role. Each channel chooses
three ranked alternates by the same process. Use staggered nine-year
nonrenewable terms. Permit removal only for incapacity, serious misconduct, or constitutional
disqualification after a five-human special removal panel drawn by human
sortition finds the ground by clear and convincing evidence and both
nonappointing channels separately approve. Membership-channel approval uses the
full fixed membership, participation above one half, and majority approval of
valid votes. Governance-channel approval uses every nonconflicted ordinary
human officeholder, two-thirds quorum, and majority approval of all eligible
officeholders. Sortition-channel approval uses a twenty-one-human qualified
jury, seventeen-person quorum, and two-thirds approval of the full jury.
Abstentions count for quorum but not approval, invalid ballots do not approve,
and ties fail. If fewer than three eligible governance officeholders remain or
a channel body has a material common conflict, a twenty-one-human jury drawn
from nonconflicted members substitutes under the sortition rule. Require recusal for personal, financial,
appointing-party, prior-participation, or material-dependency conflicts.
Whole-body compromise activates a temporary nine-human tribunal. Each channel
uses its ordinary process to choose three nonconflicted members and three ranked
alternates, excluding current or former judges and case parties. Same-channel
alternates fill recusals. Six members constitute quorum and six affirmative
votes give a final judgment subject only to `RD-03` exceptional reopening. The
tribunal then dissolves.

**Decision required:** Court size; exact membership, governance, and sortition
selection; qualified-pool eligibility, disclosure, review, and appeal;
alternates; term and staggering; removal grounds, panel, evidence burden, and
the three named approval bodies with exact participants, quorums, denominators,
votes, and conflict substitution; recusal; and special-tribunal composition,
selection, exclusions, alternates, quorum, decision threshold, finality, and
dissolution.

## DECISION-RD-03 — Jurisdiction, Appeal, Finality, Enforcement, and Remedies

**Canonical recommendation SHA-256:** `5377a2df3ca277341fc4b6d89335899254f632b44dfe18fc0ddcb6c44703bd0c`

**Alternatives tested:** Single-instance automatic enforcement; multiple broad
appeals with court supervision; one bounded appeal with enumerated authority and
separate enforcement.

**Recommendation:** Give the Court jurisdiction over ordinary constitutional
acts, emergency compliance, succession and return, office eligibility and
removal, enforcement noncompliance, effective-power challenges, and
constitutional-change classification. Exclude institutional ends and creation
of amendment, refounding, emergency, succession, or sovereign authority. Use
three-human first-instance and five-human disjoint appellate panels; one appeal
is final, with reopening only for fraud, concealed conflict, or decisive
previously unavailable evidence. Permit necessary and proportionate stays,
invalidation, suspension, remand, disclosure, restoration, restitution, and
reliance-protective prospective relief. A separate human Enforcement Office
executes final judgments and an independent Verification Office reports
compliance. Noncompliance permits disclosure, expedited review, executor
replacement, restoration, and bounded accountability.

**Decision required:** Jurisdiction and exclusions; panels; appeal and finality;
reopening; remedies; enforcement; verification; noncompliance.

## DECISION-RD-04 — Emergency Trigger, Eligibility, Prohibitions, and Expiry

**Canonical recommendation SHA-256:** `a1ac01ddd31bac8c0f98a0b28a12ed8465395a5c4e4bb9beb023b21c7b9162a3`

**Alternatives tested:** Predelegation only; reasonable-belief human fallback;
clear-and-convincing evidence with bounded concurrence and hard expiry.

**Recommendation:** Require clear and convincing contemporaneous evidence for
every `CDR-002` trigger. Limit eligibility to natural persons with direct
situational competence, no material conflict, and no role in manufactured
unreachability. Require two materially independent eligible humans when delay
permits; unilateral action is limited to cases where concurrence delay
materially increases imminent irreversible harm. Require contact through every
registered authority and delegate using two independent channels unless
physically impossible. Absolutely prohibit amendment, refounding, sovereignty
or succession creation, self-renewal, precedent creation, record destruction or
concealment, obstruction of review or restoration, and action beyond minimum
stabilization. Expire at the first controlling expiry event and never later than
twenty-four hours without valid ordinary or predelegated authorization.
Repeated use triggers interregnum review and never renews fallback authority.

**Decision required:** Evidence burden; eligibility; independence and
concurrence; contact rule; absolute prohibitions; expiry ceiling; repeated-use
transition.

## DECISION-RD-05 — Emergency Review and Remedies

**Canonical recommendation SHA-256:** `714b26396210987c61e160f32c0a684d9bb089d7fd0ccb89491a269f31e6bfc5`

**Alternatives tested:** Advisory administrative review; full ordinary
adjudication; automatic expedited constitutional review.

**Recommendation:** Require filing immediately and no later than six hours after
stabilization, with human constitutional review beginning within twenty-four
hours. The actor bears clear-and-convincing burdens for every trigger and limit.
Concealed or destroyed evidence creates a rebuttable adverse inference, not an
automatic merits judgment. Apply the `RD-03` remedy and appeal architecture;
preserve outcome neutrality. Irreversible effects remain reviewable through
disclosure, restitution, compensation, institutional correction, and
nonprecedential findings.

**Decision required:** Filing and review times; burdens and adverse inference;
standing; remedies; appeal; enforcement; irreversible-effect treatment.

## DECISION-RD-06 — Succession Proof, Surviving Grants, and Process

**Canonical recommendation SHA-256:** `e7a1af84f9bf5df96c56969cf15277255d4674b62034863dace19035e5205a02`

**Alternatives tested:** Single designated successor; succession council;
claimant-neutral continuity with separated evidence, council judgment, Court
review, and refounding referral.

**Recommendation:** Require clear and convincing proof of temporary incapacity
and beyond-reasonable-doubt proof of death, permanent loss, identity, return, and
successor identity from at least three materially independent sources. Shared
issuer, operator, data, infrastructure, incentive, or artificial-intelligence
dependence defeats independence. Establish a seven-human Succession Council:
two selected by constitutional membership, two by ordinary human offices, two
by the Court without current adjudicators serving, and one by human sortition.
Five votes determine triggers or recognize a lawful predesignation; six votes
refer impossibility of succession to explicit refounding. Require conflict
recusal and one Court appeal. Only valid current ordinary grants not personal to
the lost authority survive; foundational, fallback, expired,
revocable-on-incapacity, and succession-creating grants suspend.

**Decision required:** Proof burdens and independence; council composition and
votes; recusal and appeal; recognition scope; surviving grants; suspended
powers; refounding referral.

## DECISION-RD-07 — Interregnum Duration, Return, Restoration, and Invalid Succession

**Canonical recommendation SHA-256:** `77c069d5ca7f8df5ac38b90a1362bf1b44abfbd560343cde0bb626c83d45692e`

**Alternatives tested:** Indefinite continuity; short automatic refounding
deadline; reviewed stages with a hard constitutional ceiling.

**Recommendation:** Review interregnum at ninety days and impose a 180-day
maximum on continuity authority. The ceiling triggers a human succession
determination or explicit refounding process but never transfers sovereignty.
Surviving grants expire at their original expiry or the ceiling, whichever is
earlier. Final verification of return immediately restores authority without
custodian consent; only a narrowly tailored Court stay may prevent conflicting
irreversible acts. Invalid succession ends prospective authority, while
completed acts receive act-specific review for reliance, beneficiary protection,
reversibility, and constitutional harm. Apply invalidation, restoration,
restitution, disclosure, executor replacement, and reliance-protective
prospective treatment without validating the succession.

**Decision required:** Review and maximum duration; grant expiry; return and
restoration; stay boundary; invalid-succession effects and remedies.

## DECISION-RD-08 — Amendment and Refounding Process

**Canonical recommendation SHA-256:** `29666bcf9ee8ed33a19a1f8949c4eddceec89f746a9237deb15c449672d4ef9b`

**Alternatives tested:** Unified constituency with escalating threshold;
separate constituent assemblies; tier-matched membership voting with repeated
ratification and independent classification.

**Recommendation:** Use `RD-01` membership fixed before proposal initiation for
amendment and the same membership without delegation for refounding. Ordinary
amendment requires participation above one half and two-thirds approval after
fourteen days' notice. Structural amendment requires two-thirds participation,
three-quarters approval, and two votes at least thirty days apart; each vote
also requires majority approval in each of three equal cohorts assigned by
publicly auditable human sortition before deliberation and administered by
materially independent authorities. Refounding requires three-quarters
participation, four-fifths approval, two votes at least ninety days apart, and
an explicit discontinuity ballot. It also requires separate majority
confirmation by affected human beneficiaries: natural persons outside fixed
membership whose existing legal rights, obligations, access to institutional
assets, or eligibility for material institutional benefits would be directly
altered. The Verification Office determines and publishes that roll from public
criteria before the first vote. Any member or claimed affected person has one
expedited Court appeal, and voting cannot begin until timely challenges are
final. Confirmation requires participation above one half of the final roll and
approval by more than one half of valid votes cast. Abstentions count only for
participation, invalid ballots count toward neither denominator, and ties fail.
Require attributable human sponsorship, public
text, plain-language effects, alternatives, dissent, machine-assistance
disclosure, deliberation, and Court classification before voting. Every member
has standing; one expedited appeal is final. Emergency, succession, and
adjudication cannot waive the process.

**Decision required:** Fixed `RD-01` constituencies; participation and approval
thresholds; repeated votes and waiting periods; three sorted and independently
administered cohort majorities; affected-beneficiary definition and pre-vote
roll verification and appeal; confirmation participation and valid-vote
denominator; abstention, invalid-ballot, and tie rules; sponsorship; notice;
deliberation; classification; challenge; appeal; effectiveness; non-waiver.

## DECISION-RD-09 — Cumulative Effect and Semantic Drift

**Canonical recommendation SHA-256:** `357862d23e316e6fe9274afe1744753932071e76c66ae1a800b34a2d35c6ff24`

**Alternatives tested:** Fixed rolling window; lineage and dependency
aggregation; hybrid window, lineage, effects, and periodic drift review.

**Recommendation:** Aggregate changes within twenty-four months and older acts
linked by purpose, dependency, sponsorship, financing, infrastructure, or
necessary operational effect. Include amendments, interpretations,
implementation acts, delegation changes, and material effective-power shifts.
Any time, lineage, dependency, or combined-effects trigger initiates Court
review before further effect. Escalate for material change to office allocation,
checks, amendment conditions, protected agency, beneficiaries, sovereignty,
constitutional constraint, or another refounding-level principle. The proponent
bears clear and convincing evidence for lower-tier treatment. Permit suspension,
remand, consolidated reclassification, renewed tier-compliant approval,
restoration, disclosure, and reliance-protective prospective relief. Require a
human-led public semantic-drift audit every two years; material drift triggers
review, not automatic amendment.

**Decision required:** Aggregation units and window; linkage and effects
triggers; escalation subjects; burden; standing; timing; remedies; periodic
audit.

## DECISION-RD-10 — Termination and Refounding Transition

**Canonical recommendation SHA-256:** `3afe29ca1971592119b9edb2dcd4ee5b3da97ba04663c38ab45c798e1d7fb0c3`

**Alternatives tested:** Extinguish prior order and obligations; continue all
institutions and obligations; terminate identity with bounded preservation and
transition.

**Recommendation:** A valid `RD-08` refounding act terminates prior
constitutional identity only at an effective time after final result
certification and final disposition of every timely challenge. Before the second vote,
each of the Court's three materially independent selection channels publicly
selects two Transition Authority members and two ranked alternates, and the
constitutional membership selects one member and two ranked alternates.
Current judges, sponsors, and prior continuity custodians are ineligible, and
the final ballot identifies the slate. Refounding cannot take effect unless the
Verification Office certifies all seven members and at least seven alternates
before voting closes; challenges receive one expedited Court appeal before
result certification. The refounding act conditionally creates the Authority's
mandate, which activates prospectively when the old identity terminates. Five
nonrecused members are a quorum and five affirmative votes authorize action.
Recusal creates a vacancy filled from the relevant ranked alternates. With fewer
than five members, only record and asset preservation approved unanimously by
at least three available nonrecused members is authorized; with fewer than
three, no action is authorized. The Authority expires after 180 days, is
strictly nonrenewable, and cannot participate in creating a later
transition body. The Authority may preserve records,
safety, assets, ordinary administration, and claims, but may not amend, refound,
select ends, transfer sovereignty, irreversibly dispose of assets outside
preservation, or veto new authority. Lawful obligations
presumptively survive unless impossible, identity-dependent, or expressly
reconsidered by the new order with reasons and affected-party review. Assets
remain in custodial trust for human beneficiaries. Protect good-faith reliance
consistent with agency and beneficiary interests; deny capture-based reliance.
Permit restoration, restitution, compensation, claim continuation,
invalidation, disclosure, and custodian removal. Preserve all prior text,
decisions, dissent, claims, and transition records permanently.

**Decision required:** Post-certification termination event; pre-effective selection,
certification, ballot disclosure, conditional activation, alternates, recusal,
five-member quorum and vote, sub-quorum preservation, and minimum concurrence;
transition composition, strictly nonrenewable duration, powers, prohibitions,
review, and removal;
obligations; assets; reliance; remedies; permanent history.

## DECISION-RD-11 — Effective Power, Coalition Capture, and Machine Domination

**Canonical recommendation SHA-256:** `5f33b14236b8cdb3d6d9450dae8e8ed63854f324d53a4022bd215c03f19c753c`

**Alternatives tested:** Transparency alone; formal separation and concurrence;
layered substantive independence, disclosure, substitutability, audits, and
remedies.

**Recommendation:** Treat actors and channels as independent only absent common
control and material dependency in evidence, data, models, infrastructure,
identity, communications, financing, agenda, enforcement, and incentives.
Disclose every material dependency and prohibit any validator, model,
credential, dashboard, issuer, evidence store, communication path, or operator
from being the sole gatekeeper. Require attributable human reasons,
alternatives, dissent, authority scope, clock, review path, and
machine-assistance disclosure for material decisions. Substantive machine
domination exists when responsible humans cannot independently understand the
material basis, reconstruct reasoning, reject the recommendation, select a
viable nonmachine alternative, or control timing and effect. Human signatures
do not cure domination. Require annual independent human effective-power audits
and event-triggered audits for dependency change, coalition evidence, repeated
emergency action, prolonged interregnum, adjudicative drift, or change
concentration. Permit disclosure, recusal, substitution, suspension, remand,
restoration, diversification orders, and jurisdictionally bounded invalidation.

**Decision required:** Material independence; sole-gatekeeper prohibition;
decision disclosures; machine-domination test; audit triggers; standing;
remedies.

## Cross-Domain Reconciliation

1. **Human sovereignty versus binding review:** `RD-03` enumerates review powers
   and excludes institutional ends and creation of authority.
2. **Emergency speed versus evidence and review:** `RD-04` preserves a
   seconds-critical unilateral path and `RD-05` supplies expedited review.
3. **Continuity versus effective succession:** `RD-06` limits surviving grants
   and `RD-07` supplies reviewed stages and a hard ceiling without automatic
   sovereignty.
4. **Classification versus adjudicative amendment:** `RD-03` limits the Court to
   classification while `RD-08` reserves authorization to human membership.
5. **Discontinuity versus obligations and reliance:** `RD-10` terminates identity
   while presumptively preserving lawful obligations and assets for review.
6. **Finality versus reversibility:** `RD-03` combines one appeal with bounded
   reopening, stays, restoration, and reliance-sensitive remedies.
7. **Plurality versus correlated capture:** `RD-11` tests material independence,
   gatekeeping, substitutability, and substantive human control.
