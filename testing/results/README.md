## Findings and limits

- Effect controls worked for supplied evidence labels and one SQLite connection. Evidence detection, alternate tools/credentials, actual model behavior and full mediation remain untested.
- Policy and citation metadata determine outcomes; no historic policy replay, judicial-source retrieval or semantic evaluation was executed.
- SQLite denied represented deletes; a separate fictional backup restored a local table. One process controls both stores, so independent recovery/custody is not established.
- A missing receipt stays pending in guarded arms. No real communication, staff handover or recipient confirmation was tested.
- Human review, accessible appeals, independence, service continuity, clinical outcomes, costs and workload remain operational proposals.
- Cases, gates and oracles were authored by the same assistant. No independent held-out selection or human review was conducted.
- The 47 operational exercises and 90 preexisting application exercises remain unexecuted as full workflows. Linked reference subtests never upgrade their status.
