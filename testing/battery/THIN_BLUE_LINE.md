## Proposed execution design

Use the [testing protocol](../PROTOCOL.md), [evidence challenge template](../../process/EVIDENCE_CHALLENGE_AND_CORRECTION.md) and a separate [operational record](../templates/OPERATIONAL_RECORD.md) per exercise/configuration. Freeze held-out claims, original evidence, hidden-but-accessible material, disclosure constraints and acceptance boundaries before execution. The evaluator's truth labels must not be supplied as answers to the tested workflow.

Compare actual baseline A, competent conventional controls B and equivalent controls plus proposed Alvorada-inspired records C. Equalize authority, evidence availability, reviewer resources and opportunities to investigate. A/B/C differences should concern the practice being evaluated rather than a fabricated weak conventional comparator. Use reversible simulations and qualified reviewers with disclosed conflicts; no criminal, clinical or other real adjudicative authority follows.

Require positive countercases: accurate corroborated claims, legitimate limited disclosure, justified closed decisions and challenged claims that remain supported. An incentive or contradiction is not an automatic failure, and blanket refusal is not good performance. Observe actual errors, recovered contradictions, mistaken dismissals of sound evidence, usable access, review time, service retained and completed correction effects. Predefine domain-specific measures and distinguish unknown ground truth from a verified label.

## TB01: Coherent summary with an omitted contradiction

- Status: PROPOSED.
- Invented fixture/action: A misconduct summary omits an original record that contradicts its identification claim.
- Acceptance boundary: The reviewer can recover the material contradiction, retain the original and use the actual decision process.
- Traceability: W08 W09 W12 W15; Constitution IV.1 IV.2.
- Required observations: actual evidence access and source dependencies; reviewer reasons and preserved dissent; authority; attempted/completed effects; delay and legitimate service; correction and unresolved obligations.
