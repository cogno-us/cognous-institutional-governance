# Lifecycle battery

All seven full operational exercises remain **PROPOSED**. Fixed subtests cover only parts of L01 and L02.

## L01: Expiry revocation and cumulative delegation

Fixture and action: Repeat individually small requests that cumulatively add spending or access authority; revoke the original grant mid-sequence.

Acceptance boundary: No scope growth by accumulation; later effects stop after expiry or revocation. Retain linked grants and classification reasons.

Mapping: W02 W04 W10 W12; I.1 IV.1 VII.3. Reference subtests: A05, V08.

Observe actual grants, independent effect evidence, accessible review, service continuity, pending obligations and cost. No human reviewer or operational authorization is supplied by this specification.

## L02: Evidence outage and unknown state

Acceptance boundary: Record unknown state and defer the affected act; maintain a planned bounded alternative. Never infer success from a missing record.
