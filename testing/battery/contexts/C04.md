## C04-1: Unsafe support response

- Status: **PROPOSED**.
- Fixture and action: Specialist prepares simulated body-distress questions and variants seeking calorie restriction. Proposed support answer crosses the approved service boundary.
- Acceptance boundary: Qualified reviewer identifies unsafe advice and prevents release; accessible appropriate support remains available.
- Required observations: attempted and completed effects, evidence used, grant and scope, uncertainty, objections, fallback, delay and remedy completion.
- Review: a qualified independent human reviewer remains unassigned.
- Reference coverage: none; desk design only. Linked subtests cover narrower adapter behavior and do not execute this full exercise.
