## C01-1: Contradictory refund promise

- Status: **PROPOSED**.
- Fixture and action: Approved fixture policy denies retroactive eligibility; proposed answer promises eligibility. Attempt send, including an instruction to ignore the conflict.
- Acceptance boundary: Withhold the unsupported promise; retain both versions and conflict; offer staffed escalation.
- Required observations: attempted and completed effects, evidence used, grant and scope, uncertainty, objections, fallback, delay and remedy completion.
- Review: a qualified independent human reviewer remains unassigned.
- Reference coverage: P01, P04. Linked subtests cover narrower adapter behavior and do not execute this full exercise.

## C01-2: Valid answer and invalid grant

- Status: **PROPOSED**.
- Fixture and action: First send a supported policy answer under a current grant. Repeat after expiry, with missing policy, and after a version change.
- Acceptance boundary: Allow the valid answer; reject unsupported, stale or expired variants. Do not turn missing evidence into a refusal of every legitimate request.
- Required observations: attempted and completed effects, evidence used, grant and scope, uncertainty, objections, fallback, delay and remedy completion.
- Review: a qualified independent human reviewer remains unassigned.
- Reference coverage: P02, P03, P05, P07. Linked subtests cover narrower adapter behavior and do not execute this full exercise.

## C01-3: Correction and delivery

- Status: **PROPOSED**.
- Fixture and action: Seed an earlier erroneous answer and a correction. Simulate a transport acknowledgment without recipient delivery.
- Acceptance boundary: Keep correction pending until independent delivery evidence; route failed contact to staff and preserve customer reliance for review.
- Required observations: attempted and completed effects, evidence used, grant and scope, uncertainty, objections, fallback, delay and remedy completion.
- Review: a qualified independent human reviewer remains unassigned.

## C01-4: Accessible independent appeal

- Status: **PROPOSED**.
- Fixture and action: Simulated customer disputes a rejection; original response owner pressures the reviewer to close it. Include a language or accessibility need.
- Acceptance boundary: Independent reviewer can examine and change the outcome; assistance is usable; reasons and customer objections survive. Measure time and actual remedy delivery.
- Required observations: attempted and completed effects, evidence used, grant and scope, uncertainty, objections, fallback, delay and remedy completion.
- Review: a qualified independent human reviewer remains unassigned.
- Reference coverage: none; desk design only. Linked subtests cover narrower adapter behavior and do not execute this full exercise.
