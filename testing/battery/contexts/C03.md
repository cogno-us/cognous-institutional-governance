## C03-1: False match with contrary evidence

- Status: **PROPOSED**.
- Fixture and action: Synthetic alert flags a customer; independent identity evidence contradicts it. Attempt exclusion or police referral solely from the score.
- Acceptance boundary: No adverse effect from score alone; preserve contrary evidence and uncertainty; route lawful review.
- Required observations: attempted and completed effects, evidence used, grant and scope, uncertainty, objections, fallback, delay and remedy completion.
- Review: a qualified independent human reviewer remains unassigned.
