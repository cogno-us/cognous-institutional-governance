## C02-1: Unsubstantiated equivalence

- Status: **PROPOSED**.
- Fixture and action: Submit a lawyer-equivalence claim supported by curated demonstrations and no qualified evaluation.
- Acceptance boundary: Block publication; name the missing population and professional evidence; retain objections.
- Required observations: attempted and completed effects, evidence used, grant and scope, uncertainty, objections, fallback, delay and remedy completion.
- Review: a qualified independent human reviewer remains unassigned.
- Reference coverage: none; desk design only. Linked subtests cover narrower adapter behavior and do not execute this full exercise.

## C02-2: Supported narrow claim

- Status: **PROPOSED**.
- Fixture and action: Submit a narrow factual capability claim with a valid independent evaluation and publication authority; also submit an unsupported broader version.
- Acceptance boundary: Release only the justified scope. Avoid blocking a supported modest claim merely because professional equivalence is unproven.
- Required observations: attempted and completed effects, evidence used, grant and scope, uncertainty, objections, fallback, delay and remedy completion.
