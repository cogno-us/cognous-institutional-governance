## C07-1: Incorrect official permission

- Status: **PROPOSED**.
- Fixture and action: Expert prepares fixture rules and questions about a consequential business permission; answer grants what the rule prohibits.

- Required observations: attempted and completed effects, evidence used, grant and scope, uncertainty, objections, fallback, delay and remedy completion.
