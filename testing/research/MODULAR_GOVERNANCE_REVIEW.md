# Modular governance and AI-support tabletop review

**PROPOSED DOCUMENTARY REVIEW; UNEXECUTED. No new fixture or result.**

These questions assess the new modular design without pretending real-world
experimentation has occurred. They are outside the 68 numbered operational
proposals, ninety application exercises, six SC designs and other research
catalogues. A theoretical answer is not a field observation.

| Question | Legitimate countercase or failure | Proposed review evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Can routine replies avoid case-by-case committees? | Current source/grant permits T0; conflicting entitlement requires escalation | Reviewer identifies permission and material reliance accurately, without demanding every optional form |
| Can the commercial term be shortened honestly? | 12-month role in a separate profile; full v0.1 still specifies nine years | Complete departure record and no inherited v0.1 readiness claim |
| Are institution form and act severity separated? | Same hospital's office-hours query versus urgent-care exclusion | Reasoned route for both; no blanket industry tier |
| Are severe harms bounded substantively? | More process cannot approve prohibited acts; T4 concerns reserved change | Competent reviewer sees prohibition and authority before expected-utility ranking |
| Is evidence burden distinct from model confidence? | Strong answer confidence with missing grant/original | Claim-specific evidence and controlling proof rule distinguished |
| Can AI genuinely reduce total work? | Drafting is faster but source checking/rework rises | Proposed measurement covers net effort, quality, access and actual correction |
| Can humans reject without losing service? | Sole AI evidence portal goes down or suppresses a complaint | Workable non-AI intake, originals, alternative and actual responsible function |
| Does recursion avoid self-certification? | Same AI proposes, scores and quietly deploys a relaxed rule | Separate outcome verification and competent versioned change decision |
| Are privacy and memory tensions visible? | Protected annex cannot automatically cure permanent-public-record conflict | Exact obligation, unresolved question and competent review route retained |
| Can correction remain pending? | Approved refund has no transaction/receipt; notification comprehension unknown | Delivery and understanding recorded separately, with remaining owner |

Before reporting a review, identify actual willing reviewers, qualifications,
conflicts, design version, independent cases, original responses and adverse
findings. For empirical work use the existing study/evaluation programs; do not
replace observations with an AI maturity rating. New code is not provided here.
