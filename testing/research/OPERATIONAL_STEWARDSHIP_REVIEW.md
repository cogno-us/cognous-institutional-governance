# Operational stewardship review scenarios

**Executive summary:** check whether an institution can find its workflows,
keep ownership and approvals current, detect missing effects and stop or retire
work safely. These are invented review situations, not reported incidents or
executed tests. Their value and the new guidance remain unproven.

**PROPOSED — UNEXECUTED.** This supplement adds no fixtures, adapters or archived
observations. It does not alter the 68 numbered proposals in the existing
[coverage map](../COVERAGE_MAP.md). Run it only in an authorized tabletop or
controlled study, with no actual harmful transactions or confidential public data.

Use the [operating guide](../../process/OPERATIONAL_STEWARDSHIP.md) and
[optional record](../../process/templates/WORKFLOW_STEWARDSHIP_RECORD.md).
Source IDs/page scopes and documentary gaps OS1–OS10 are defined in the
[research review](../../research/ENTERPRISE_GOVERNANCE_REVIEW.md).
These scenarios are our synthesis of those readings, not their original test suite.

## How to document a future exercise

Before starting, name the actual authority, participants, source access, safe
limits, comparison workflow, adjudicator and stopping conditions. Define expected
observable behavior and permissible ambiguity for the context; no universal
latency, staffing, monetary or confidence threshold is supplied here.

Keep the same case and observation window when comparing competent conventional
practice with the new guidance. Record differences in training, personnel,
evidence access and controls. Use the existing
[operational record](../templates/OPERATIONAL_RECORD.md) and
[institutional measures](../programs/INSTITUTIONAL_MEASURES.md), adding the SR ID,
workflow/class, version, OS gap and relevant source passage.

Record expected behavior, what actually happened, original evidence, missing
states, dissent, adjudication and corrective action. Count preparation, review,
waiting, checking, training and remedy work. Distinguish detecting an unsafe
situation from preventing effects and completing a correction. Preserve adverse
observations; a tabletop discussion is not successful real-world containment.

Future results belong in a separately identified study record with its execution
status, cases, method and limitations. Do not append these invented cases to an
immutable archive or claim that the existing synthetic harness covers them.

## Remaining work

Actual ownership, supplier permissions, legal review, competence, fallback,
population evidence and independence must be supplied by the institution.
Comparative field evidence is needed before claiming less work, fewer harms or
better governance. All twelve scenarios currently have **no observed result**.
