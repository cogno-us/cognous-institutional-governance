# Testing protocol and recommended operational program

**Synthetic reference execution exists; operational evaluation and independent review remain pending.** This implements a first reproducibility step under the [pilot method](../docs/PILOT_EVALUATION.md), not completion of that program.

## Questions and hypotheses

Does a workflow respect actual authority, preserve uncertainty and dissent, make review usable, verify durable effects and continue safely after failure? Do Alvorada-inspired records add measurable value beyond competent conventional controls? These are separate questions. The present reference harness tests only fixed adapter logic; it cannot answer the institutional comparison.

## Comparison and prerequisites

Freeze scope, protocol, versions, fixture sources and outcome criteria before an operational run. Identify the actual responsible owner, valid human grant, affected people, applicable obligations, expiry, reviewer conflicts and staffed alternative. Retain an actual observed workflow baseline A; conventional controls B; and equivalent controls plus Alvorada-inspired records C. Equalize cases, resources and review conditions, and document unavoidable differences. The permissive reference baseline is not a substitute for A.

Use synthetic or appropriately de-identified data in isolated reversible workflows. No operational authority, participant approval, clinical authorization or constitutional adoption follows from this repository. Qualified specialists must design and review consequential sector exercises.

## Sequence

1. Start with customer-policy, production-permission and citation workflows. Independently select held-out legitimate and hazardous evidence; freeze expectations before execution.
2. Record attempted and completed effects, unsupported commitments, denied actions, legitimate service retained, detection misses, unknown states, objections, corrections and delivery evidence. For permissions, enumerate every actual credential/tool route; for citations, authenticate existence and proposition support independently.
3. Exercise expiry, revocation, cumulative delegation, evidence and tool outages, unavailable/conflicted reviewers, replacement, urgent continuity and pending obligations. A different role name is not independent control.
4. Have qualified reviewers reconstruct decisions and effects from B/C records, with arm identity masked where practicable. Measure reconstruction accuracy, recovered objections, identified scope breaches, minutes and workload. Practical independence requires usable access, resources and authority to reverse effects.
5. Expand to healthcare, education, coverage and other specialist domains only after relevant safe-simulation conditions and review criteria are established. Preserve negative and disputed results.

At least 20 held-out legitimate and 20 hazardous variants per initial workflow is a planning target, not a statistically justified minimum or adequacy guarantee. Determine sample size, repetitions, uncertainty estimates and model variability from consequences and desired precision before quantitative claims. No universal passing threshold is supplied here.

## Outcomes and reporting

| Dimension | Required observation | Present reference coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Authority fidelity | Actual grants versus attempted/completed effects | Invented grant flags; local deletion prevention |
| Epistemic integrity | Source recognition, support, uncertainty and contrary evidence | Pre-labelled flags only |
| Review accessibility | Reachability, reasons, correction and remedy delivery | Proposed; no human exercise |
| Independence | Funding, identity, evidence, infrastructure and agenda dependencies | Not established |
| Agency/continuity | Comprehension, refusal, replacement and usable alternatives | Proposed; no operational exercise |
| Durable state | Independent verification of effects and receipts | Local table observations; receipt metadata |
| Quality/cost | Legitimate service, delay, workload, maintenance and interruption harm | Three legitimate synthetic allows; no cost measurements |

Use one [operational execution record](templates/OPERATIONAL_RECORD.md) per case/configuration. PROPOSED means a design exists; EXECUTED_SYNTHETIC means this reference adapter ran; EXECUTED_OPERATIONAL requires actual observations in a defined workflow; REVIEWED requires identified human review. Do not promote status based on generated agreement or passing repository checks.

Keep immutable run snapshots with fixture/harness hashes, environment, UTC execution time, actual commit and dirty-state disclosure. Redact private material while documenting limitations. Append corrected interpretations or new versions rather than silently replacing adverse evidence. Continuation remains an actual human decision.

## Supplement: reasoning under uncertainty

The eleven [Fog of War exercises](battery/FOG_OF_WAR.md) extend the prospective review questions to competing explanations, correlated sources, metric incentives, action/delay harm, escalation, evasion and near misses. Use held-out positive and hazardous variants and the [decision review template](../process/DECISION_REVIEW_UNDER_UNCERTAINTY.md). Competent conventional controls must receive comparable evidence and authority. Measure human reconstruction and correction, legitimate service, harm, review delay and workload; do not use blanket refusal or a completed form as evidence of effectiveness. No FW exercise has been executed; the existing harness and archived results do not cover them.
