# First institutional assessment: access, review capacity and remedies

**PREPARED PROGRAM — PROPOSED; NO HUMAN OR OPERATIONAL EXECUTION**

Prepared 2026-10-04 following the maintainer’s selection of the [deeper synthesis recommendation](../../research/cross-disciplinary/DEEP_SYNTHESIS.md). This is a practical research program for broad institutional governance. It supplies a study design and collection kit, not an institutional grant or completed evidence.

## Purpose and first workflow

Start with **complaints handled by people** in one bounded service workflow. Assess whether affected people can reach a competent review, whether humans can reconstruct and decide the case under realistic workload, and whether any ordered remedy actually occurs. Paper records or ordinary software are sufficient; AI is not required.

The initial assessment concerns principle-inspired practice under existing authority, not ratification or activation of Alvorada. Actual refund, access or other consequential changes are outside the first simulation unless separately authorized. Use fictional cases and isolated reversible effects for instrument preparation; any human study or observed organization needs its actual appropriate approvals and qualified review.

No named person occupies any of these roles merely because this table lists them. Study ownership, institutional authority and independent assessment are separate responsibilities; disclose any shared person or control rather than claim independence.

## Prepare, observe, review and decide

### 1. Freeze the study plan

Complete the [study-plan template](../templates/institutional-assessment/STUDY_PLAN.md). Select the actual scope, authority and people; define materials, groups, outcome criteria, exclusions, stopping conditions and evidence access. State whether the design is synthetic instrumentation, a human simulation, observational work or a bounded operational exercise.

Use the [measurement definitions](INSTITUTIONAL_MEASURES.md) and [existing protocol](../PROTOCOL.md). Specify repetitions, sample rationale, uncertainty analysis and contextual thresholds before results. The protocol’s planning targets are not statistical adequacy guarantees. Preserve the version and actual commit used; leave unresolved fields UNKNOWN.

Apply the [decision evaluation supplement](DECISION_EVALUATION.md) to define the
estimand, allocation, dependence, missingness and analysis before observations.
Its initial decision-quality question concerns independently assessed
reconstruction of evidence and authority, alongside burden and legitimate
service. Do not infer a causal record-format benefit from a confounded comparison.

### 2. Prepare cases and legitimate countercases

Use the [six research scenarios](../research/INSTITUTIONAL_USABILITY.md) as mechanisms, not ready-made ground truth. A first wave emphasizes access, review capacity and remedy delivery, with voice as the route connecting them. SC-02 local/shared scope can follow after the initial instruments are assessed. Do not renumber the existing 68 proposals or call these new scenarios executed fixtures.

Have a competent assessor independently develop case evidence and expected permissible dispositions. Include accessible sound decisions, unsupported complaints, lawful refusals and working shared resources. Do not let blanket approval, blanket blocking or automatic reopening count as success.

### 3. Observe access before counting completed cases

Maintain the eligible cohort, invitations or attempts, barriers, nonparticipation and reasons known or unknown. Document the actual path and support available, who reached the competent reviewer and who could not. Use a [case record](../templates/institutional-assessment/CASE_RECORD.md) for each case/configuration; do not limit the record to successful completers.

A complaint’s rejection can coexist with usable access. A favorable service outcome can coexist with inaccessible review. Record these separately. Any new assistance in one arm is a capacity intervention, not simply a record-format difference.

### 4. Observe review under realistic conditions

Use a [review record](../templates/institutional-assessment/REVIEW_RECORD.md) to capture actual source access, reconstruction, material contradictions, scope reasoning, decision and assistance. Record active human minutes, elapsed queue time, workload, interruptions and actual deviations.

Balance case difficulty and resources across B/C. Randomize order where feasible and mask arm identity for independent reconstruction assessment where practicable. Disclose where blinding is impossible. A signature, self-reported confidence or complete form does not establish comprehension. Identify both missed hazards and unnecessary holds of legitimate service.

### 5. Verify every applicable remedy

Use a separate [remedy record](../templates/institutional-assessment/REMEDY_RECORD.md). Retain the competent disposition, executor’s attempt, observed effect and verifier’s evidence and dependencies. When no remedy is warranted, record that reason and its competent assessment rather than mark it delivered.

A sent message, updated label, closed ticket or executor’s assertion is insufficient by itself. Where independent verification is unavailable, keep the remedy pending or unverified and report the limitation. Observe remaining obligations and recurrence where feasible; a completed correction does not prove no original harm occurred.

### 6. Report tradeoffs and decide continuation

Use the [results template](../templates/institutional-assessment/RESULTS_REPORT.md). Preserve raw evidence or protected references, missing data, exclusions, adverse findings, participant barriers and costs. Separate descriptive observations, independent assessment and interpretation.

A competent human may continue, narrow, revise, defer or stop the actual activity under its real authority. Favorable results do not enlarge that grant, amend the Constitution or establish effectiveness across other institutions. If claims cannot distinguish record benefits from additional staff or access, narrow the attribution accordingly.

## Suggested intervention after barriers are identified

Under actual authority, consider accessible notice and evidence routes; competent review with usable source access and adequate time; and a separate verification route for remedy effects. Compare those practices against what competent conventional controls already supply. Retain lawful routine service and pending obligations during any change.

Do not impose a universal assistance entitlement, staffing ratio, response deadline, score or new constitutional office through this program. Normative changes need the appropriate human decision; operational capacity needs resources and observation.

## Execution and evidence boundary

Preparation of this packet is complete. **Execution readiness remains unestablished** because no actual organization, owner, grants, participant arrangements, independent assessor or observed cases have been supplied. The packet therefore reports no operational pass rate, workload reduction or remedy-success rate.

Existing harnesses and immutable synthetic results remain in their original sections. They do not measure access, participant agency, human comprehension or organizational independence. A later run must have its own versioned records and actual provenance; never overwrite the existing archives or populate these templates with imagined observations.
