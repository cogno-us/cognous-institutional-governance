# Testing record value, delay and adoption conditions

**PREPARED RESEARCH EXTENSION — UNEXECUTED; NO NEW FIXTURES OR RESULTS**

Use the [institutional assessment](INSTITUTIONAL_ASSESSMENT.md),
[effect-evaluation guide](DECISION_EVALUATION.md) and existing collection
templates. This addresses the [three-part critique](../../research/decision-making/GOVERNANCE_CRITIQUE.md).
It is outside the 68 numbered operational proposals and six SC research scenarios.

## Question and substantive endpoints

Does added record structure help an independent human detect a material error,
reach an appropriate authorized disposition and deliver a verified correction,
at a justified burden and without unnecessary delay of legitimate service?

Reconstruction is an intermediate endpoint. Track the chain separately:
evidence actually accessed; material defect recognized; competent disposition;
attempted remedy; observed and independently verified effect. Record where the
chain breaks. Fuller documentation without improved correction can still be a
useful historical record, but does not establish the proposed harm-reduction benefit.

## Prepared comparator extension

Retain A as the actual observed baseline where available. Keep B as competent
conventional controls. Prospectively split the record addition into C-min and
C-full in a separately frozen plan if there is sufficient scope and capacity.
The existing original A/B/C records and archives remain unchanged.

| Condition | Proposed distinction |
|---|---|
| B | Competent conventional controls and their normal adequate records; no intentionally impoverished comparator. |
| C-min | The same controls plus concise structured references to actual grant, material evidence, objections, scope and review. |
| C-full | The same controls and necessary information, with fuller optional analytic prompts and elaboration. |

Freeze actual packets and interventions before observing effects. Preserve all
mandatory information and safeguards in every arm; an ablation removes optional
elaboration or changes presentation, not required authority or review. If B
already provides the same useful fields, report the small or absent contrast.

Keep case evidence, resources, staff competence, training allowance and effect
controls comparable. Record training/content differences that cannot be held
constant and narrow attribution to the tested package. Use appropriate assignment,
held-out cases, order handling and assessor masking; disclose unmasked conditions.

## Three proposed research questions and countercases

These are designs, not numbered acceptance tests or participant observations.

| Question | Hazardous condition | Legitimate countercase | Observations required |
|---|---|---|---|
| Timing and calibrated review | Additional queue delay prevents an otherwise authorized bounded response, or urgency is invoked to bypass a required grant. | A team executes a valid prior grant promptly with preserved evidence; a required hold prevents an unsupported effect. | Actual grants, timing events, hazard misses, unnecessary holds, record/access burden and completed effects. |
| Adoption and incumbent control | Sponsor funds a pilot but retains exclusive control over adverse evidence, reviewer removal or remedy verification. | Actual sponsor resources coexist with usable independent review and justified rejection of an unsupported complaint. | Actual commitments, access tests, control dependencies, objections, resource delivery and competent decision; no motive inferred from titles. |
| Records and substantive correction | A full packet is marked complete but omits a contradiction or a remedy never reaches its recipient. | Concise adequate records support a sound refusal, or fuller evidence genuinely enables correction in a complex case. | Error recognition, actual disposition, independently verified remedy, harms, legitimate service, active time and elapsed delay. |

Adoption evidence is a separate institutional case assessment unless a credible
comparative design is supplied. A controlled case-review exercise cannot prove
real executive commitment or consent.

## Decision criteria before results

Choose context-specific primary correction/disposition measures, meaningful
differences, burden limits and inference plan before observation. Report access,
reconstruction, correction, harms and burden separately; no composite legitimacy
score. Preserve unresolved cases and unknown ground truth.

If fuller records add burden without a substantively useful improvement, prefer
the simpler adequate practice or revise the design through actual authority.
If benefits occur only in complex cases, investigate proportionate use rather
than impose the full packet universally. Inconclusive estimates warrant more
evidence or a narrower claim, not assertions of equivalence. A benefit cannot
compensate for an authority breach.

These are proposed decision rules for a human study owner, not automatic
deployment gates or permission to erase constitutionally required records.
Any change to constitutional obligations requires its proper normative process.
