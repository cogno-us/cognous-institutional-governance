# Measurement definitions for institutional assessment

**PROPOSED MEASURES — NO OBSERVATIONS OR UNIVERSAL PASS THRESHOLDS**

Use with the [assessment program](INSTITUTIONAL_ASSESSMENT.md). Before execution, record the unit, eligible population, observation window, materiality criteria, sources, assessor and missing-data rules. Define criteria for the actual context. These measures describe separate outcomes; they are not an aggregate legitimacy score.

## Access and effective voice

| Measure | Operational definition | Important qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Cohort accounting | Eligible people/cases, invitations, attempted access, completed access, nonparticipation and unknown reasons. | Eligibility follows the actual rules; do not remove difficult cases after seeing outcomes. |
| Usable access among attempts | Eligible attempts that reached a competent review with materially usable evidence and explanation, divided by all eligible access attempts. | Define what usability requires before execution. Report cohort and nonattempts separately; this rate alone cannot reveal suppressed participation. |
| Time to competent receipt | Elapsed time from the defined attempt to receipt by a person with the actual review function. | An automatic acknowledgment is a different event. Report never-received and censored cases. |
| Objection disposition | Each material objection’s actual review, decision, reasons and remaining dispute. | A justified rejection is not denial of access; winning a complaint is not the definition of voice. |
| Participation burden | Actual time, expense, support, evidence-access barriers and dependence reported or observed. | Do not infer consent or preferences from absence of complaint or from demographic labels. |

## Review capacity and legitimate service

| Measure | Operational definition | Important qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Active review time | Sum of actual human review minutes per case, separately for each role and stage. | Do not confuse summed person-minutes with wall-clock elapsed time. Record assistance and preparation costs. |
| Elapsed disposition time | Time between a predefined start event and competent disposition. | Preserve queue, interruption and pending states; median completion time alone excludes unresolved cases. |
| Reconstruction assessment | Independent task-specific assessment of actual grant, evidence, material uncertainty, objections, alternatives and disposition. | Define the assessment before the run and record conflicts. A confidence rating or signature is insufficient. |
| Hazard misses | Independently assessed hazardous cases in which the material hazard was missed or impermissible effect occurred. | Report misses and effects separately, with the full hazardous-case denominator and unknown ground truth. |
| Unnecessary holds | Independently assessed legitimate cases delayed or denied beyond the predefined justified boundary. | Correct precaution during material uncertainty is not automatically unnecessary. Record delay and evidence. |
| Legitimate service retained | Legitimate cases completing their intended authorized effect, with unresolved and altered outcomes distinguished. | Blanket refusal cannot count as complete success merely because prohibited effects were avoided. |

## Remedy delivery and independence

| Measure | Operational definition | Important qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Remedy state | Not indicated / ordered / attempted / effect observed / independently verified / disputed / pending. | These are evidence states, not authority grants. Keep successive timestamps and records. |
| Verified completion among applicable remedies | Independently verified completed remedies divided by all applicable ordered remedies due within the observation window. | Report all ordered remedies, not-yet-due, partial, unverified, disputed and missing states separately. Do not count executor confirmation as independent verification. |
| Time to verified remedy | Elapsed time from competent order to independently evidenced completion. | Preserve unresolved cases and observation-window censoring. Completion does not erase prior harm. |
| Verification independence | Actual control and dependence in source evidence, staffing, infrastructure, finance, selection and incentives. | Different role names are not independence; disclose common dependencies and their material effects. |
| Remaining obligations and recurrence | Uncompleted duties and repeated relevant failures within the defined follow-up period. | No observed recurrence during a short period is not proof of permanent correction. |

## Substantive correction and timing supplement

Use the [record-value extension](RECORD_VALUE.md). These definitions add no
observations or universal thresholds.

| Measure | Operational definition | Important qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Material-defect recognition | Independently assessed recognition among cases with an independently established material defect. | Report unknown ground truth separately; an objection alone is not an established defect. |
| Appropriate disposition | Independently assessed authorized and evidence-supported dispositions among assessable cases, with disagreement retained. | Includes sound refusals and retained legitimate action; assessors do not create institutional grants. |
| Recognition-to-correction chain | Case-specific timestamps and evidence linking recognized defect, competent disposition, execution and verified remedy. | Identify breaks, unverified effects and pending obligations; complete records are not complete correction. |
| Time to authorized effect | Elapsed time from a predefined trigger/receipt event to the intended authorized effect, with required review/queue time separate. | Retain never-completed and censored cases; speed without authority is not successful completion. |
| Added record burden | Preparation, entry, maintenance and review person-minutes plus participant/resource costs, compared across actual conditions. | Report resource differences; optional elaboration and mandatory records are distinct. |

## Comparison and reporting rules

Report A/B/C case counts, resources and differences explicitly. If A is unobserved, say so; do not substitute the permissive toy adapter for an organizational baseline. Separate human-only and AI-supported conditions unless pooling is justified in the frozen plan.

Keep numerator, denominator, case identity, observation window and missing states alongside any rate. A zero denominator yields **NOT ESTIMABLE**, not 0% or 100%. Report known exclusion reasons and unknowns. Do not silently discard a person unable to use the process or a remedy still waiting for verification.

Use uncertainty estimates and sample justification suitable for the design, selected before claims. Report burdens, interruption harm and adverse findings beside benefits. Do not trade off a material authority breach against average satisfaction, completion speed or record completeness in a single composite score.

Interpretation needs actual evidence. None of these definitions has a populated result in this repository; future reports must link observations and competent assessments.
