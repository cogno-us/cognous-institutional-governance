# Film studies: judgment, evidence and correction

**Executive summary:** the films show why confidence and institutional authority
should not end inquiry. Check competing explanations, preserve contrary evidence
and verify that a correction changes people's circumstances.

| Study | Simple lesson | Detailed analysis |
|---|---|---|
| The Fog of War | Test assumptions, consider harm and reconsider escalation | [Eleven themes, sources and limits](FOG_OF_WAR.md) |
| The Thin Blue Line | A coherent story can be wrong; inspect originals and deliver correction | [Ten analytical themes, source distinctions and court record](THIN_BLUE_LINE.md) |

These are later comparative readings. They distinguish film presentation,
attributed testimony, historical/judicial sources and this repository's
interpretation. They do not show that Alvorada would have prevented the events.
