repository currently supports, what remains uncertain, and how to improve the

## Method and review limitations

The research uses AI assistance in analysis, drafting, and repository work.
Controlling human design decisions depend on the explicit provenance recorded
in the decision files, not on AI-generated agreement. Review independence must
be demonstrated rather than inferred from the number of models or passes.
No independent external review board, field-validation program, or institutional
activation is established by this repository.

Historical analogy can generate questions and counterexamples but cannot prove
transferability. Shared data, models, funding, infrastructure, and assumptions
can correlate nominally separate reviews. Office separation, voting rules,
records, and nonmachine alternatives may be formally present while practically
unusable. Institutional legitimacy and enforcement also depend on the affected
people, applicable law, incentives, resources, and implementation.

## Priority research work

1. Verify historical claims at passage level, preserving contested interpretation
   and contrary evidence. Upgrade only the entries actually verified.
2. Obtain independent human critique of authority allocations, inclusion,
   representation, accessibility, conflict procedures, and cumulative change.
3. Evaluate one bounded workflow against a stated baseline, using the
   [pilot method](../docs/PILOT_EVALUATION.md). Report quality and cost alongside
   unauthorized effects, agency, review access, and concentration of control.
4. Test practical replacement and evidence reconstruction without the contested
   provider controlling the exercise. Include outage and reviewer-vacancy cases.
5. Study maintenance burden, recruitment, participation, and timely review before
   making claims about feasibility of full constitutional adoption.

Claims should expand only when their supporting evidence expands. The immediate
public contribution is an inspectable research architecture and reusable
questions; empirical efficacy, market demand, and broad institutional resilience
remain open.

## Broad institutional scope and prepared assessment

The framework’s current scope includes human-only, software-supported and AI-enabled institutions; broader scope is not broader performance evidence. The [prepared assessment](../testing/programs/INSTITUTIONAL_ASSESSMENT.md) prioritizes access, review capacity and remedy completion in a human-run workflow. Its actual owner, institutional grants, participant arrangements, independent assessor and observations remain unfilled. Current synthetic findings must not be generalized to these human or institutional outcomes.
