## Selection method and remaining work

Searches combined the named institutions with decision analysis, strategic
judgment, evidence-based management, experimentation, organizational learning
and policy evaluation. Publisher and author/institution sources were preferred.
We selected mechanisms that add something usable to the existing reviews, and
included a methodological counterargument rather than treating one econometric
tradition as universally sufficient. Search snippets were used to locate works,
not to claim full-text review.

This leaves substantial gaps: systematic replication review, forecasting and
calibration, negotiation, multi-party preference aggregation, implementation
costs and non-Western business research. Before claiming effectiveness, obtain
full licensed practitioner texts where needed, broader empirical evidence and
independent assessment of the actual workflow. Institutional names and author
prestige cannot substitute for those steps.
