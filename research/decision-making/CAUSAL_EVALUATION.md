# Causal evaluation: what changed because of the decision?

**METHODOLOGICAL SYNTHESIS — NO NEW EFFECT ESTIMATE**

See [sources and access limits](SOURCES.md). Econometrics is useful for testing
effects of institutional practices. It does not decide whose objectives are
legitimate or turn an estimated benefit into authority.

## Contributions and disagreement

**DM08 — Angrist and Pischke.** Their methodological argument places research
design at the center of credible empirical inference. For Alvorada, more rows
or more elaborate analysis cannot rescue an unidentified comparison.

**DM09 — Athey and Imbens, Stanford.** Their review addresses causal
identification, regression discontinuity, synthetic controls, supplementary
checks and external validity. The inspected earlier paper separates statistical
precision from identification and discusses placebo and sensitivity analyses.
A well-estimated association need not be a causal effect, and a local effect
need not transfer to another institution.

**DM10 — Ludwig, Kling and Mullainathan.** They distinguish mechanism
experiments from evaluations of realistic policies. This helps frame future
research questions. The existing deterministic harness is an instrument check,
not an empirical behavioral mechanism experiment.

**DM11 — Nevo and Whinston.** Their counterargument defends credible structural
as well as nonstructural analysis. For an untried institutional arrangement,
explicit models may explore consequences, but predictions depend on their
assumptions. They are not observed field effects.

## Our recommendation for study selection

Start with the decision-relevant effect, population, comparison and observation
window. Select a method that fits the actual assignment and data, not the most
impressive label. These are brief orientation points, not an estimation manual.

| Approach | Credibility question |
|---|---|
| Randomized comparison | Was allocation implemented, and did attrition, noncompliance or shared reviewers compromise the contrast? |
| Observational comparison | What makes the groups comparable, and which relevant differences remain unobserved? |
| Regression discontinuity | Is the threshold-based comparison credible, and how local is the conclusion? |
| Synthetic control | Is the comparison history adequate, and could other contemporaneous changes explain the result? |
| Structural model | Which assumptions generate the prediction, and what evidence or alternative model challenges them? |

The rows draw on DM08–DM11; implementation needs qualified methodological
review. A before/after difference alone does not isolate record-format effects
from staffing, case mix, training or changing policy. Repeated observations of
one reviewer are not automatically independent observations of many reviewers.

## What to preserve in this repository

Use the [decision evaluation plan](../../testing/programs/DECISION_EVALUATION.md)
to freeze hypotheses, primary measures, allocation, exclusions and analysis
before observing outcomes. Keep effect sizes, uncertainty, harms, burden and
missing observations visible. Select robustness checks that address an actual
assumption; passing checks cannot prove every identifying assumption.

The first useful contrast remains competent conventional controls versus the
same controls with Alvorada-inspired records. If one arm also receives extra
staff, easier access or training, identify the tested package or separate that
intervention. Otherwise the added value of the records remains unresolved.

The archived synthetic configurations deliberately share effect controls.
Their identical fixture outcomes establish neither superiority nor statistical
equivalence in institutions. No hypothesis about human decision quality has
been executed by adding these research documents.
