## Source-supported starting point

The [Stanford Encyclopedia’s Arendt study](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/arendt/) discusses plurality, political action, speech and judgment. It is a scholarly interpretation rather than a primary-source review of her complete corpus.

## Interpretation for Alvorada

Preserve the possibility of people acting and judging together. A collection of optimized outputs is not a political community, and an administrative procedure cannot determine everyone’s ends. Records should retain distinct positions rather than manufacture one institutional voice.

Relevant existing provisions and principles: **I.1; II.2; IV.1; W03, W09, W13**. This mapping is the repository’s interpretation; it does not claim original influence, amend the proposal or confer authority.

## Limits and counterargument

Plural participation does not automatically produce agreement, correct evidence or safe decisions. A deliberative ideal still needs authority boundaries, protection for nonparticipants and accountable execution. These are present applications, not Arendt’s proposed AI architecture.
