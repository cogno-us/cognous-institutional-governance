## Source-supported starting point

His [2000 BMJ article Human error: models and management](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1117770/) distinguishes person-focused explanations from examination of systemic conditions, active failures and latent weaknesses in defenses. This directly inspected research article is a conceptual account, not a new experiment performed here.

## Interpretation for Alvorada

Investigate recurring conditions behind an error: workload, interfaces, procedures, incentives and dependent safeguards. Preserve incident and near-miss reporting and verify that correction changes the conditions that permitted harm.

## Limits and counterargument

System analysis does not erase individual responsibility or excuse deliberate abuse. Multiple nominal barriers may fail together because they share evidence, infrastructure or incentives. Counting controls is insufficient evidence of safety.
