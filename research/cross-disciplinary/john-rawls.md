## Source-supported starting point

The [Stanford Encyclopedia’s original-position study](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/original-position/) explains a hypothetical choice situation in which particular social positions are withheld to assess fair principles. This is a philosophical construction, not an observed ratification procedure.

## Interpretation for Alvorada

Test a rule from positions its designer does not occupy: newcomer, minority, complainant, subordinate and person bearing its costs. Require an account of why the arrangement is defensible beyond the advantaged actor’s interests.

Relevant existing provisions and principles: **II.2; IV.1; VII.2; W07, W13**. This mapping is the repository’s interpretation; it does not claim original influence, amend the proposal or confer authority.

## Limits and counterargument

A hypothetical agreement is not actual consent. Impartial framing can hide disputed assumptions about people, values and resources. The exercise cannot authorize adoption or require Alvorada to accept Rawls’s entire theory of justice.
