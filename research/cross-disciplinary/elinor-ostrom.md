## Interpretation for Alvorada

Investigate multiple centers of bounded decision-making, local participation and accountable monitoring rather than assume either centralized control or unrestricted decentralization solves governance.

## Limits and counterargument

Local groups can exclude people or externalize costs. Multiple centers create coordination and escalation burdens. Commons research does not establish that any particular AI institution will work, and mechanisms require case-specific study before adoption.
