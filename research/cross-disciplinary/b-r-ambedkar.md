**LATER COMPARATIVE NOTE — NO CONSTITUTIONAL EFFECT**

Added 2026-10-04. Category: Political leadership. This is a scoped initial study, not a biography, full literature review or empirical validation. See the [method and synthesis](README.md).

## Source-supported starting point

His [25 November 1949 Constituent Assembly speech](https://www.constitutionofindia.net/debates/25-nov-1949/) warns against political hero worship and separates political equality from social and economic inequality. The transcript is a primary record reproduced by the Centre for Law and Policy Research’s Constitution of India project. It documents his argument, not proof of later outcomes.

## Interpretation for Alvorada

Constitutional participation needs material conditions that let people use it. Challenge founder exceptionalism, including a benevolent founder’s claim that personal judgment can replace institutional limits.

## Limits and counterargument

A written constitution cannot itself remove entrenched inequality. His defense of constitutional methods should not be turned into an automatic prohibition of protest or a claim that existing remedies are always available and sufficient.
