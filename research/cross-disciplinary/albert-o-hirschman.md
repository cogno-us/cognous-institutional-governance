## Source-supported starting point

The [Harvard University Press description of Exit, Voice, and Loyalty](https://www.hup.harvard.edu/books/9780674276604) distinguishes leaving an organization from seeking improvement within it. ## Interpretation for Alvorada

Provide a usable challenge route and examine whether departure is realistically possible. Customer switching, employee resignation and member withdrawal do not have the same costs or consequences. Silence is not evidence of satisfaction.

## Limits and counterargument

Exit can remove knowledge needed for reform, while voice can be costly or ineffectual. Loyalty must not be demanded as a waiver of challenge. The interaction needs fuller primary-text review and context-specific evidence.
