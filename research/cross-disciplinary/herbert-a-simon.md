## Source-supported starting point

His [Nobel lecture](https://www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2018/06/simon-lecture.pdf) describes selective search and satisficing within bounded rationality. The [award explanation](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/1978/press-release/) rejects the assumption of an omniscient maximizing decision-maker. Indexed passages were available; direct full-text retrieval was blocked.

## Interpretation for Alvorada

Design for limited attention, information and time. Present decision-relevant alternatives and uncertainty in a form an accountable human can actually inspect. A nominal approval step can fail if workload makes independent judgment infeasible.

Relevant existing provisions and principles: **IV.1–IV.2; W11, W15**. This mapping is the repository’s interpretation; it does not claim original influence, amend the proposal or confer authority.

## Limits and counterargument

A feasible stopping rule is not permission to ignore material harm or authority requirements. AI assistance may reduce some burdens while adding dependence and verification work. Human feasibility must be observed, not inferred from a completed form.
