## Source-supported starting point

The [Yale University Press description of Seeing Like a State](https://yalebooks.yale.edu/book/9780300078152/seeing-like-a-state/) presents his critique of imposed schematic plans that disregard complex relationships and local practical knowledge. This is publisher-level orientation, not a full verification of each historical case.

## Interpretation for Alvorada

Treat categories, dashboards and standardized evidence as selective representations. Preserve the person’s account and the local conditions that a system cannot fit into its fields. Ask whose interests become invisible when an institution simplifies.

## Limits and counterargument

Standardization also supports coordination and equal treatment. Local practices are not automatically benign. The lesson is to test omissions and enable correction, not to reject measurement, planning or common rules wholesale.
