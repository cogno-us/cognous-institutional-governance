## Source-supported starting point

The [Nobel account of Sen’s welfare research](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/1998/press-release/?app=true) distinguishes resources from actual opportunities or capabilities; his [autobiographical account](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/1998/sen/biographical/) connects capabilities and a broader informational basis for justice. Indexed passages were retrievable, but direct page retrieval was blocked.

## Interpretation for Alvorada

Measure whether a person can actually use a right or service, rather than infer access from an entitlement, income proxy or available interface. An appeal must be understandable, reachable and feasible for the affected person.

Relevant existing provisions and principles: **I.1; II.2; IV.2; W07, W15**. This mapping is the repository’s interpretation; it does not claim original influence, amend the proposal or confer authority.

## Limits and counterargument

Capability assessment requires choices about relevant opportunities and evidence; it does not supply a universally agreed score. Access review must not become paternalistic substitution of the institution’s preferred ends for the person’s own.
