## Source-supported starting point

The [Stanford Encyclopedia’s Weber study](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/weber/) discusses rationalization, bureaucracy and authority. This scholarly account supports conceptual orientation, not a claim that every rule-bound administration is legitimate.

## Interpretation for Alvorada

Separate competence, office, personal loyalty and justification of authority. Rules and records can make actions predictable, but their existence does not establish that the underlying power is rightful. Inspect the control exercised through administrative expertise.

## Limits and counterargument

Description of why people obey is different from justification of why they should. Bureaucratic consistency can preserve unjust practices or concentrate effective power. The remedy is not simply more paperwork or charismatic replacement.
