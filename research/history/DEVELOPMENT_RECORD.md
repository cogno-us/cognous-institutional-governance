# Development record: what to retain and how to reconstruct it

**Executive summary:** To reconstruct the design, follow the decisions into requirements, the draft and its review. Older questions describe earlier stages; they are not automatically current blockers.

[Read the detailed analysis](#detailed-analysis) or return to the
[research overview](../README.md).

## Detailed analysis

This index separates current proposal and status from preserved analyses and
future proposals. It is a reading aid with no constitutional effect. The
[chronology](README.md) gives dates; [decision rationale](../../process/DECISIONS_AND_RATIONALE.md)
explains alternatives, reasons, and costs.

## The shortest reconstruction path

1. Read the five [foundational human decisions](../../constitutional-design/decisions/README.md)
   and their alternative architectures, review, and dissent.
2. Follow their [35 accepted requirements](../../constitutional-design/requirements/README.md)
   into the [20-group consolidated map](../../constitutional-design/CONSOLIDATED_REQUIREMENT_MAP.yaml).
3. Read CDD-001 through CDD-004 in the decision index for coordinated corrections,
   membership and selector architecture, the College lifecycle, and Schedule O.
4. Follow the [section traceability](../../constitutional-design/drafts/CONSTITUTION_v0.1_TRACEABILITY.yaml)
   into [Constitution v0.1](../../constitutional-design/drafts/CONSTITUTION_v0.1.md).
5. Examine the [final review](../../constitutional-design/RATIFICATION_REVIEW_v0.1.md)
   and [ratification packet](../../constitutional-design/RATIFICATION_PACKET.md).
   Text-level readiness and actual human ratification remain separate.

## Results and proposals mean different things

Candidate stress tests and constitutional review findings remain because they
show why alternatives were rejected, where gaps were found, and how later
choices addressed them. A failed candidate case is not automatically a failure
of the final proposal. A passed paper-review case is not empirical evidence that
an operating institution resists capture.

The [capture catalogue](../../constitutional-design/organic-governance/CAPTURE_TAXONOMY_AND_TESTS.md)
and [ninety application exercises](../../applications/README.md) are proposed and
unexecuted. Repository tests verify encoded invariants and integrity; they do
not execute these sector exercises. [IR-19](../../constitutional-design/future-research/IR-19-synthetic-moral-status.md)
and the [governed-agency work package](../../constitutional-design/organic-governance/GOVERNED_AGENCY_DESIGN_REQUIREMENTS.md)
remain separate future proposals.
