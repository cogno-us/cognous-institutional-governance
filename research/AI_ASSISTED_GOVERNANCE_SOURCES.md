Review date: 2026-10-04. This is selective supporting research, not an exhaustive
review, external endorsement or evidence that the new modules work. Source
passages were accessed through primary publishers. No external source text is
relicensed as original Alvorada material.

## Design inference, explicitly separate from evidence

The shared charter/profile/procedure architecture, T0–T4 labels, illustrative
short terms, minimum functional arrangements and bounded recursive learning
process are **Alvorada design proposals**. Their exact configuration is not
prescribed by these sources. Existing W11 supplies the proportional-governance
principle; the new module makes an operational interpretation explicit.

The practical hypothesis is that AI can lower preparation costs and improve
access to evidence while humans focus on meaningful choices. Whether that
hypothesis holds depends on checking/rework costs, task fit, source quality,
rights, independence and observed correction. Source breadth cannot replace
actual evaluation. More source verification, counterargument and independent
institutional review remain needed.
