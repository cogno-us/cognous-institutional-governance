# The Fog of War: institutional lessons for Alvorada

**SUPPORTING ANALYSIS — NO CONSTITUTIONAL EFFECT**  
Added 2026-10-04. This applies Errol Morris's 2003 documentary *The Fog of War: Eleven Lessons from the Life of Robert S. McNamara* to an existing design. It is a later comparative reading, not evidence that the film shaped the original decisions.

## Source and interpretation boundary

The [director's transcript](https://www.errolmorris.com/film/fow_transcript.html) supplies the eleven numbered lessons and their context. Their themes are paraphrased below. The [official film description](https://www.sonypictures.com/movies/thefogofwar) identifies the subject and historical scope. The [Brown University teaching guide, hosted by Sony](https://www.sonyclassics.com/fogofwar/_media/pdf/lessonPlanFOG.pdf) treats the account as contestable retrospective testimony. Sources checked 2026-10-04.

This is not a comprehensive historical or legal review. McNamara's recollections and Morris's editorial framing are evidence about the film's account; they are not final findings about responsibility, necessity, casualty estimates, or what would have happened under another institution. No claim is made that Alvorada would have prevented a war or incident. The application columns and procedures are this repository's analysis, not statements attributed to the filmmakers.

## What this changes in practice

The useful addition is a review procedure, rather than another statement that leaders should exercise good judgment. Alvorada already allocates authority and preserves reasons. The following proposed practice makes those obligations easier to examine before and after consequential action:

1. **Separate what was observed from what was inferred.** Link original records, dates, uncertainty and contrary evidence. Document whether apparently separate reports share a supplier, model, witness or data source. Multiple sources can still share incentives; provenance alone does not establish truth.
2. **Make the alternative explanation consequential.** Ask what observation would favor it, what evidence is missing, and whether a narrower action remains justified under both explanations. An AI-generated opposing view is a hypothesis, not independent human review.
3. **Set limits before escalation.** Identify the responsible human, actual grant, excluded acts, expiry and conditions for narrowing or stopping. Repeated small approvals must be assessed together for practical authority growth. New grants require the competent authority; an expired grant cannot be renewed by relabeling the same act.
4. **Assess harm as well as output.** Record adverse effects on each affected class, irreversibility, service interruption and the cost of review. A faster workflow with more erroneous exclusions has not demonstrated an improvement merely because it completes more cases.
5. **Keep challenge practically available.** Record who can obtain evidence, challenge a premise, reach an alternate reviewer and cause an authorized correction. A nominally independent reviewer sharing the disputed evidence gatekeeper may be unable to act independently.
6. **Review near misses and fortunate outcomes.** Separate the observed outcome from whether the decision was supportable using the evidence and authority available at the time. Success cannot retroactively create authority; an adverse outcome alone cannot establish invalidity. Preserve unresolved disagreement.

Use the [decision review template](../process/DECISION_REVIEW_UNDER_UNCERTAINTY.md). Routine low-consequence work can use a short record; consequential and irreversible cases need a proportionate assessment. This tailoring does not waive any fixed constitutional procedure.

| Add a usable decision record and domain examples | An abstract historical analogy is insufficient for delegation, challenge, correction and harm assessment. |

## Transfer limits and evaluation

Military crises, customer complaints and clinical decisions differ in stakes, evidence, mandates and time available. Transfer the questions about authority, uncertainty and review; do not equate the cases or import military framing into civilian services. Domain files contain specific examples.

The [supplementary exercises](../testing/battery/FOG_OF_WAR.md) test whether the proposed procedures would remain usable under time pressure, misleading corroboration, metric incentives, moral rationalization and reviewer dependence. They are unexecuted proposals. The existing reference harness receives pre-labelled evidence and does not model these institutional dynamics. Comparative value requires observed conventional and Alvorada-inspired workflows, independent human review, and measurements of decision reconstruction, harm, delay and legitimate service retained.
