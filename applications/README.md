# Applications

**ILLUSTRATIVE APPLICATION GUIDANCE — NO CONSTITUTIONAL EFFECT**

Alvorada can inform settings where institutions exercise consequential power,
including human-only, conventional-software and AI-supported arrangements. These eighteen context files translate its
principles into decisions, grants, records, review, continuity, and withdrawal.
Each context covers **all 17 working principles, all 10 protected foundational
commitments, and all 8 constitutional Articles**. The
[principle catalogue](PRINCIPLE_CATALOGUE.md) maps these to all 24 sections.

Customer response concerns what an organization communicates; customer service
concerns how it resolves cases and changes accounts. Clinical healthcare and
healthcare administration have different decision rights. Government service
delivery and policy formation require different mandates. Military operational
support and logistics likewise separate command accountability from resource
administration. Cross-cutting enterprise-agent and cybersecurity files help
examine shared tools without collapsing these domain distinctions.

## Choose the form of application

1. **Principle-inspired practice:** improve an existing organization's delegation,
   evidence, review, and human control under its own lawful authority.
2. **Institutional adaptation:** evaluate a separately authorized architecture,
   with explicit constituencies, independence, and sector constraints.
3. **Full constitutional adoption:** satisfy v0.1's actual human membership,
   seven-human College, nine-human Court, distinct Verification and Enforcement,
   Schedule O, selection chains, thresholds, ratification, and activation.

A CEO, clinician, commander, minister, customer-service supervisor, or donor is
not automatically Human Constitutional Authority. A complaints team is not
thereby the Court. These analogies do not waive office sizes, fixed procedures,
standing, human-only allocations, emergency limits, or change rules. The current
Constitution remains **proposed for human ratification, not adopted**.

## What every context contains

Each file identifies beneficiaries and affected people, ordinary authority,
review roles, candidate support uses, and consequential decision boundaries.
Its W table covers every working principle, including explicit uncertainty,
dissent, effective power, comprehensibility, and durable state. Its F table
covers human ends and beneficiaries, sovereignty, binding constraints,
non-accretion, human agency, adjudication/amendment/refounding distinctions,
and preserved history. W16 and W17 are applied carefully as operational and
repository analogies; the Constitution mandates no specific transaction or
remote-storage protocol.

Each file then applies all eight Articles, follows a decision from proposal to
correction, examines affective, competence, and normative capture, and proposes
five acceptance exercises. These **90 proposed exercises have not yet been
evaluated**. Repository-validation tests verify different claims and must not
be presented as evidence that these sector workflows work in deployment.

## Method for a bounded evaluation

1. Specify the service purpose, affected people, applicable obligations, and real
   human source of authority. Distinguish sector law from constitutional analogy.
2. Inventory actions and consequences. Separate proposed recommendations,
   bounded routine execution, and consequential or expanded-scope decisions.
3. Assign separate authorization, evidence, review, execution, and verification
   functions. Disclose common control, dependencies, conflicts, and funding.
4. Make uncertainty, alternatives, dissent, reasons, scope, expiry, and
   corrections accessible in durable records. Test independent comprehension.
5. Exercise contestation, emergency boundaries, continuity, withdrawal, and a
   viable nonmachine alternative before increased reliance. Use the context's
   proposed cases and preserve failures as evidence.
6. Have competent humans decide the next grant or withdrawal. Review linked
   changes and cumulative power rather than treating each small expansion as
   harmless. Preserve earlier decisions and unresolved objections.

## Evidence and scope limits

These are public governance examples. They publish no private implementation
mechanism, executable agent runtime, clinical treatment advice, tactical
military instructions, targeting process, or physical operating specification.
They do not establish legal compliance or grant operational authority. Sector
references and evidence limitations are in the [catalogue](PRINCIPLE_CATALOGUE.md).

Domain pilots require their own competent assessment, applicable approvals,
independent observations, and human decisions. Preserve a clear difference
between a proposed control, an executed exercise, a reviewed result, and an
adopted institutional obligation.
