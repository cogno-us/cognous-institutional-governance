# Routing existing applications through modular governance

**Illustrative routing; not automatic classification, adoption or sector approval.**

Use a [profile](../constitutional-design/modular/README.md) for structure/terms
and [proportional governance](../process/PROPORTIONAL_GOVERNANCE.md) for each act.
The eighteen original context files and ninety exercises remain intact. This
routing table and the AI module add no executed application result.

| Existing context | Starting profile; pair as needed | Lower-burden example | Stronger route example |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Customer response](customer-response.md) | Commercial | T0 current opening hours | T2+ disputed refund entitlement or harmful reliance |
| [Customer service](customer-service.md) | Commercial | T1 clearly eligible reversible refund | T2/T3 denial affecting essential access |
| [Sales and commerce](sales-marketing.md) | Commercial | T0 sourced product information | T2/T3 sensitive manipulation or substantial contractual commitment |
| [Finance](financial-services.md) | Commercial + safety-critical | T0 general source-backed information | T3 consequential credit, suitability or financial rights decision |
| [Clinical healthcare](healthcare-clinical.md) | Safety-critical | T0 nonclinical administrative information | T3 competent clinical decision with applicable duties |
| [Healthcare administration](healthcare-administration.md) | Commercial/public + safety-critical | T1 routine scheduling | T3 delay or exclusion affecting urgent care |
| [Public services](government-public-services.md) | Public | T0 form explanation | T2/T3 benefits denial, sanction or exclusion |
| [Policy and rulemaking](government-policy.md) | Public | T0 research organization | T2/T3 policy effects; T4 protected mandate/architecture change |
| [Military operational support](military-operational-support.md) | Safety-critical | T0 permitted non-tactical document lookup | T3 potentially lethal/coercive effect; prohibited acts remain prohibited |
| [Military logistics](military-logistics.md) | Safety-critical | T0 inventory query | T3 safety-critical readiness/release choice |
| [Cybersecurity](cybersecurity.md) | Commercial/public + safety-critical | T1 bounded reversible isolation under real grant | T3 response with critical-service or rights spillover |
| [Employment](employment-hr.md) | Commercial/public | T0 policy explanation | T2/T3 dismissal, hiring exclusion or rights-affecting surveillance |
| [Education](education.md) | Public/community/commercial | T0 timetable answer | T2/T3 exclusion, safeguarding or consequential assessment |
| [Research](research.md) | Community/commercial/public | T0 organize cited material | T2/T3 sensitive study/publication; no invented findings |
| [Infrastructure](manufacturing-infrastructure.md) | Safety-critical | T0 maintenance documentation | T3 physical-control or critical-service change |
| [Procurement](supply-chain-procurement.md) | Commercial/public | T0 approved catalogue lookup | T2/T3 conflicted award or safety-critical dependency |
| [Enterprise AI agents](enterprise-ai-agents.md) | Commercial plus relevant domain | T0/T1 bounded granted digital action | T3 broad destructive access; T4 authority architecture change |
| [Community services](nonprofit-community-services.md) | Community + safety-critical where essential | T0 ordinary service notice | T2/T3 essential support allocation or exclusion |

Examples assume the actual lawful grant and sector constraints are known. A
low-tier label never authorizes work. AI can help prepare packets across these
contexts using [AI supporting institutions](ai-assisted-institutional-governance.md);
the [recursive process](../process/VIRTUOUS_RECURSION.md) explains how outcomes
can inform better future rules without automatic self-amendment.
