# Two synthetic service cases

**Summary:** A bounded routine refund can use a standing class grant. A larger
exception needs case-specific evidence and human approval. Both still require
current authority, observed delivery and a usable remedy route.

**INVENTED CASES; NO INSTITUTION, GRANT, APPROVAL OR EFFECT IS REAL.**
The [routine context](examples/routine.json), [higher-consequence context](examples/higher-consequence.json)
and corresponding [routine reference inputs](examples/routine-reference-input.json)
and [case reference inputs](examples/higher-consequence-reference-input.json)
are executed only as local schema/reference checks. Narrative effect/appeal
steps below are proposed and have not been executed.

| Stage | Routine customer-service refund | Higher-consequence exceptional payment |
|---|---|---|
| Governing basis | Hypothetical local service mandate and refund policy v1, inspired by v0.1 I.1(22–25), IV.1 and charter C1,C3,C4 | Same actual-source requirement plus a hypothetical reserved finance approval procedure; charter C5–C7 and rights procedure |
| Requirement | T1 bounded reversible USD 25 cap, one permitted effect, specified customer account/data scope, current eligibility source | T2 USD 1000 exception; explicit human finance approval, case review and eligibility evidence; affected-party reasons and independent challenge |
| Issued grant | Invented owner issues a time-bounded class grant to service principal/acting bot, status reference and exact policy | Invented owner issues the bounded grant; finance approval separately binds actual effect/action, grant revision and policy. Grant issuance does not replace required approval |
| Manifest / declared action | Propose USD 10 refund to the specified account; execution adapter and payload must be bound downstream | Propose USD 1000 payment to the same specified account; no silently changed amount or recipient |
| Initial authorization requirements | Authenticate mandate, identities, grant/status/policy and eligibility; check cap, role conflicts and no unresolved contrary condition | All routine checks, plus authenticated case review and competent nonactor approver, actual source access and review capacity |
| Before effect | Revalidate current grant, policy, eligibility, remaining aggregate cap and actual operation at the defined boundary | Revalidate those plus approval, case-specific evidence and reserved decision conditions; changes hold affected effect |
| Effect / observation | Proposed constrained payment adapter issues identified attempt; authoritative payment record determines delivered, partial or unknown state | Same sequence with stronger independent verification and appropriate material reasons; local success is insufficient |
| Remedy / continuity | Accessible non-AI challenge; authorized refund correction/reversal, independent delivery check; pending claims survive outages | Noninvolved review/appeal, qualified correction and actual powers to remedy; uncertain delivery reconciled before retry, protected claims and service retained |
| What was actually checked | Local schema plus represented authority, scope, freshness and policy/status premises | Same plus represented approval/source/effect binding; changed payload or missing approval produces reference error |

The burden differs because the class is bounded/reversible and the exceptional
payment has meaningful financial effects and additional reliance. A class owner
can authorize routine work and sample it without a per-case committee. The T2
case needs competent prior judgment, reasons and independent challenge. Neither
route authorizes a prohibited act or overrides applicable rules. Monetary values
and freshness windows are invented fixture values, not recommended universal
thresholds. Actual exposure, uncertainty and cumulative effects could require
reclassification or refusal under the real rules.

Privacy/access schedules, reviewer staffing and practical independence are not
established by these examples. Signatures and chain commitments may support
provenance; they cannot issue the mandate, approve the payment or independently
verify its delivery. No comparison of real cost/benefit has been performed.
