# Decision review under uncertainty

**PROPOSED PRACTICE TEMPLATE — NO GRANT, AMENDMENT OR RATIFICATION**

This template applies the [Fog of War analysis](../research/films/FOG_OF_WAR.md) to existing lawful workflows. Use it before consequential commitments and when a review trigger occurs. It supports IV.1 records and IV.2 dependency review under the proposed Constitution; it does not instantiate constitutional offices or invent sector authority.

## Record to complete

| Field | Record actual facts, reasons or an explicit unknown |
|---|---|
| Identity and decision | Record ID, version, date, responsible human, intended effect and affected people. |
| Purpose and authority | Authorized objective, actual grant source, limits, excluded acts, expiry, reviewer and applicable duties. |
| Observation versus inference | Original evidence and time; interpretations; missing facts; uncertainty; source dependencies; machine assistance. |
| Alternative account | Strongest plausible competing explanation; affected-person perspective; what would support or refute each account. Do not invent testimony. |
| Options and consequences | Proposed act, narrower/reversible alternative and non-action; benefit, harm, delay and irreversibility for each affected class. |
| Metric and incentive check | What the performance metric omits; who gains from proceeding; costs shifted to others; evidence, funding and agenda dependencies. |
| Challenge and decision | Material objections, their disposition, accessible review path, actual accountable decision and reasons. Preserve dissent that does not prevail. |
| Limits and triggers | Bounds on cumulative effects, pause/reconsideration triggers, expiry, alternate authorized reviewer and continuity arrangements. |
| Observed effects | Attempted versus independently observed completed effects; pending obligations, receipts, near misses and unexpected harm. Leave blank until observed. |
| Review and correction | Whether reasons were supportable at the decision time; actual remedy and delivery; retained prior record; superseding authority and next review. |

## Operating sequence

1. Record authority and the known evidence before selecting an action. Mark missing information UNKNOWN, not verified or safe.
2. Test the alternative explanation and examine source dependence. If uncertainty is immaterial, explain why; if material, seek clarification, narrow or defer the affected act through actual authority.
3. Have the competent human select a bounded option and record the objections. Where a reviewer is unavailable or conflicted, use an already authorized alternative or defer; an AI does not appoint a substitute.
4. Reopen the decision when a stated trigger occurs. Examples include a contradicted premise, duplicated sources mistaken for corroboration, unexpected harm, grant expiry or a newly unavailable remedy.
5. Preserve the previous record, actual effects and outstanding obligations. Correction is not deletion. Report decision quality separately from fortunate or unfortunate outcomes.

For imminent threats, distinguish valid predelegation from the narrow V.2 necessity fallback. Apply the actual trigger, burden, scope, contact attempts, expiry and review requirements. This template neither lowers them nor creates a general emergency exception. Compare harms of delay without treating urgency as permission.

## Example: customer policy claim

**Invented illustration, not an observed result.** An assistant proposes an exception based on three webpages. All three repeat the same outdated policy. An authorized complaint reviewer finds a current conflicting source and records a customer's contrary account. The organization holds the unsupported commitment, preserves ordinary help through its existing service route, and asks the competent policy owner to decide the exception. It retains the original answer and any obligation already incurred rather than silently rewriting the record. The claimed authority and remedy must come from the real organization.

## Evaluation boundary

Measure whether another reviewer can reconstruct the actual grant, contrary evidence, options, decision and completed effects. Record time, workload, missed hazards and unnecessary delays. A completed form is not proof of independent judgment or effective control. Use the [testing protocol](../testing/PROTOCOL.md) and [execution record](../testing/templates/OPERATIONAL_RECORD.md) for observed exercises.

## When a material claim is challenged

Use the complementary [evidence challenge and correction ledger](EVIDENCE_CHALLENGE_AND_CORRECTION.md) when originals, contradictory accounts, source incentives or completed remedies need examination. Its [Thin Blue Line basis](../research/films/THIN_BLUE_LINE.md) distinguishes observations from reconstructions and requires authorized, usable review access. A coherent decision narrative is not sufficient evidence of truth or delivered correction.
