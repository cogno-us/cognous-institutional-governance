# Calibrating review effort and decision delay

**PROPOSED OPERATIONAL PRACTICE — NO NEW AUTHORITY OR AUTOMATIC EXCEPTION**

Use with the [decision workflow](DECISION_MAKING.md) and
[critique assessment](../research/decision-making/GOVERNANCE_CRITIQUE.md).
Calibration concerns work and timing within actual authority. It cannot waive
required separation, records, applicable review or a binding constraint.

## Establish routes before a time-critical event

| Candidate route | Required basis and operating bounds |
|---|---|
| Routine bounded execution | Actual standing grant, defined effects and limits, competent owner, revocation, contemporaneous required evidence and usable review route. Do not add bespoke approval to each instance unless actually required. |
| Focused prior review | Material uncertainty, disputed scope, consequential irreversible effects or an actual rule requiring review; identify the competent person and required evidence. |
| Predelegated incident response | Actual prior grant with holder, scope, trigger, duration, revocation, review and restoration. In the proposal, V.1 is the ordinary emergency route. |
| Necessity fallback | Only if every V.2 condition is established; eligible human judgment, required contact attempts, minimum stabilization and V.3–V.4 limits. A delay estimate alone cannot qualify it. |

These are candidate operational routes, not new constitutional categories or
software to deploy. Specify applicable sector/institutional requirements and
actual staffing. Predelegation makes a permitted response available before a
crisis; it does not allow its holder to redefine purpose or extend the grant.

## Record a contextual timing and consequence assessment

- Actual act, responsible human, grant and feasible routes.
- Time before the material opportunity or harm changes; evidence for that window.
- Additional delay from each required review step, including queue and contact
  time, separately from active person-minutes.
- Plausible harms of action and delay, reversibility, affected groups and
  continuing obligations; separate predictions from observations.
- Material uncertainty and evidence dependence; what a review could change.
- Available authorized narrower action, alternate reviewer and continuity route.
- Contextual response target, its competent owner, evidence basis and expiry.
- Actual route chosen, reasons, required records and reconsideration trigger.

When probabilities and consequence estimates are justified, compare the
additional expected harm of waiting with the preventable harm from additional
review, and test sensitivity to plausible assumptions. Report distributional
effects separately. When estimates are weak, use scenarios, bounds or UNKNOWN.
Neither calculation nor urgency supplies permission for an otherwise excluded act.

Response targets are proposed service expectations, not constitutional
deadlines or licenses to proceed when time expires. Define what happens on a
miss: authorized alternate, narrower act, escalation or deferral, as applicable.
Do not hide an unavailable reviewer behind an artificial deadline.

## Preserve review while reducing avoidable work

Link existing evidence; remove duplicate entry and unrelated questions; prepare
standing grants and competent alternates; observe queue bottlenecks. Use parallel
work only where the required functions and dependencies allow it. A faster
workflow that quietly merges judgment and verification is not a successful
calibration.

Material dissent and uncertainty remain in the record. Optional elaboration
may be staged only where actual requirements permit; do not convert required
contemporaneous records into retrospective rationalization. The proposed
fallback expires at the earliest V.3 event, no later than 24 hours; V.4 requires
filing immediately and within six hours after stabilization and review beginning
within 24 hours after filing. This guide changes none of those limits.

## Example and countercase

**Invented design example.** A cyber-response team has an actual prior grant to
isolate a specified device class under defined conditions, with a bounded
restoration procedure. It can act within that scope and preserve evidence for
review. Destructive actions outside that grant remain separate choices. The
worksheet neither creates the grant nor demonstrates a response benefit.

**Countercase.** A manager claims urgency to avoid an available reviewer or
manufactures unreachability. A favorable expected-value calculation does not
satisfy V.2. Keep the unsupported act outside the claimed route and use the
competent authorized process.

## Observe and recalibrate

Measure time to authorized effect, required-review delay, unresolved cases,
hazard misses, unnecessary holds and verified correction. Include tail delays
where the sample supports them. Compare equal-resource conditions and record
any added capacity separately. Repeated target misses call for competent review
of grants, staffing or scope; they do not automatically weaken safeguards.

No response target or probability threshold has been validated here. The
[record-value program](../testing/programs/RECORD_VALUE.md) includes unexecuted
questions about both dangerous delay and unsupported fast action.
