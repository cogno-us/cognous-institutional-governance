# Leadership and institutional review

**PROPOSED WORKSHEET — NO AUTHORIZATION OR CONSTITUTIONAL CHANGE**

This worksheet applies the [five leadership studies](../research/leadership/README.md) to consequential decisions in an existing lawful workflow. The historical cases generate questions; they supply no office, delegation or emergency permission. Use the [uncertainty review](DECISION_REVIEW_UNDER_UNCERTAINTY.md) for the decision record and [evidence challenge ledger](EVIDENCE_CHALLENGE_AND_CORRECTION.md) for disputed claims and remedies.

## Record the answers

| Question | Evidence to retain | Relevant proposal |
|---|---|---|
| What competent human act actually permits this decision? | Grant, approving body, separate approvals still pending, scope and expiry. Keep proposed agreement separate from ratified commitment. | I.1; II.1; IV.1; VII.2 |
| Could a leader or supplier determine the review’s outcome? | Evidence access, appointment chain, infrastructure, budget and agenda dependence; usable alternative review; conflicts and costs. | II.3; III.2–III.3; IV.2 |
| Who benefits, bears harm or lacks usable standing? | Actual affected groups, access barriers, contrary accounts and lawful remedies. Mark missing testimony unknown rather than inventing consent. | II.2; IV.1–IV.2 |
| Can people reconstruct the mechanism and challenge its premises? | Accessible reasons, source-specific claims, alternatives, material dissent and protected review arrangements. Preserve setbacks alongside recovery. | IV.1; W08, W09, W15 |
| What makes urgent action permissible and bounded? | Actual trigger and grant; narrower option; costs of delay; prohibited acts; scope, expiry, competent review and continuity if the leader is unavailable. | V.1–V.4; VI |
| What separates enactment or experimentation from delivery? | Named lawful implementer, resources, observed effects, pause conditions, independent verification and outstanding obligations. Do not fill the results field before observation. | III.4; IV.1; W16, W17 |
| Does this reform cumulatively change institutional identity? | Prior changes, aggregate effects, applicable change category and actual approving acts. A small amendment is not automatically harmless. | VII.1–VII.3; VIII |

Record the accountable human’s decision and reasons for proceeding, narrowing, deferring or declining. Preserve substantive objections that do not prevail. If needed authority, information or review access is missing, use the real institution’s lawful escalation route; this worksheet cannot create a replacement authority.

## Illustrative application

**Invented customer-service example, not a test result.** A leader proposes a popular AI-led complaint pilot. Review identifies that the provider controls both the answers and evaluation data, some customers cannot use the appeal channel, and the pilot’s launch date precedes required approval. The competent organization can decide whether to narrow or defer the affected launch, provide usable access and separate evaluation, while continuing already authorized service. Document any costs, customer obligations and remaining disagreement. A favorable satisfaction metric cannot fill in missing approval or erase excluded customers.

## Evaluation status

A filled worksheet does not establish comprehension, independence or delivered correction. Future evaluation should measure review time, missed harms, unnecessary holds, preserved legitimate actions, usable challenge and actual remedy completion. Define cases and observations under the [testing protocol](../testing/PROTOCOL.md). No new case execution or efficacy finding accompanies this document.
