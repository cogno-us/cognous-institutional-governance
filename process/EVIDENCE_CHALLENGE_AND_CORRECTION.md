# Evidence challenge and correction

**PROPOSED PRACTICE TEMPLATE — NO NEW AUTHORITY OR ADOPTED LAW**

This template applies the [Thin Blue Line analysis](../research/THIN_BLUE_LINE.md) to existing lawful workflows. Use it for a material challenged claim, missing contrary evidence, suspected shared-source corroboration or a correction whose effects remain incomplete. It supplements [decision review under uncertainty](DECISION_REVIEW_UNDER_UNCERTAINTY.md).

## Authority and access record

- Record ID, case, date, version, responsible human and proposed effect:
- Actual decision authority, scope, expiry and applicable evidence standard:
- Affected people and lawful challenge, reconsideration or appeal route:
- Reviewer identity, conflicts, practical evidence access and qualified alternate:
- Custodian, authorized access, privacy limits and retention obligations:
- Protective action during review, if independently authorized; harm from delay:

Do not invent a new evidence threshold, appeal entitlement, public disclosure duty or reviewer appointment. Record UNKNOWN where facts are unavailable. Unavailability should be escalated or recorded through the competent process; it does not automatically establish wrongdoing.

## Claim and contradiction ledger

| Claim ID / material proposition | Original source / time / version | Observation, reported statement, inference or reconstruction | Support and limits | Contrary or missing evidence | Dependence, suggestion or incentive | Reviewer disposition / unresolved state |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| To complete | To complete | Label explicitly | To complete | To complete; never silently omit | To complete | To complete; not generated consensus |

Keep a conclusion separate from the individual claims supporting it. Preserve exact originals where permitted, material prior versions and the chronology of changes. Mark AI summaries and synthetic reconstructions explicitly. A source's reward, institutional standing or changed account is relevant context; none automatically determines truth. Record whether a witness or reviewer saw the institution's preferred answer before supplying an account.

## Review and correction ledger

| Stage | Record actual action and evidence |
|---|---|
| Challenge received | What was contested, by whom through the lawful route, and the material evidence. |
| Evidence access | What the reviewer received or could independently inspect; refused, missing or restricted records and reasons. |
| Decision | Competent human's disposition, reasons, applicable standard, retained dissent and unresolved findings. |
| Authorized remedy | Specific scope, responsible executor, affected records, obligations, notice and deadlines under the actual grant. |
| Completed effects | Independently observed account, eligibility, publication or other state; distinguish pending from completed. |
| Verification | Identity and dependencies of the verifier; evidence of implementation; disputed or unverified effects. |
| Supersession and follow-up | Links to preserved prior records, downstream correction status, outstanding harms and responsible owner. |

## Operating checks

1. Inspect each material claim rather than approving only a narrative summary. Preserve supporting and disconfirming evidence with its access limits.
2. Examine shared origins, exposure to suggested answers and incentives. Seek clarification without treating inconsistency as proof of dishonesty.
3. Use a competent, practically independent reviewer under the existing process. If that reviewer lacks access, record the limitation and seek an authorized alternative or defer the affected decision as appropriate.
4. Classify the disposition precisely: affirm, narrow, correct, reopen through the applicable process, defer or unresolved. New evidence is not self-executing authority.
5. Assign the remedy and confirm its actual effects through a separate usable route. Reconcile downstream copies and pending obligations; do not close the case because a summary was edited.
6. Preserve the failure and correction history under lawful custody. Publish only what is authorized and appropriate; meaningful challenge does not require indiscriminate exposure.

## Evaluation

Use [TB01–TB10](../testing/battery/THIN_BLUE_LINE.md) and the [operational execution record](../testing/templates/OPERATIONAL_RECORD.md). Measure recovery of material contradictions, unjustified adverse actions, wrongly rejected sound evidence, reconstruction accuracy, access barriers, review time, legitimate service and independently verified remedy effects. A blank or completed ledger is not a result, and a confident AI assessor is not an independent human reviewer.
