# Workflow stewardship record

**Optional template:** maintain one compact record per material workflow or
authorized routine class. Link existing records instead of copying them.
This is an unfilled template, not an appointment, authorization or result.
Use with [operational stewardship](../OPERATIONAL_STEWARDSHIP.md).

## Deeper record when material

Include only what the consequences require. Existing privacy/retention, adoption,
decision and evaluation records remain their canonical sources.

1. **Discovery and portfolio:** known coverage, undiscovered-use limitations,
   related workflows, shared providers/reviewers/evidence routes and cumulative
   affected population. Name the person responsible for refresh.
2. **Boundary and obligations:** inputs, users, sources, AI/software/human roles,
   integrations and effects; applicable instrument/source/version/verification
   date, duty, owner and review trigger. Link qualified interpretation separately.
3. **Supplier commitments:** approved features and uses, change notice, data and
   evidence access/export, incident cooperation, exit, subcontractors and unresolved
   contractual or technical limitations. Record assurance entity/product/version,
   period, criteria/exclusions and differences between promises and actual configuration.
   Link downstream rights propagation, preservation decisions and verified limits.
4. **Responsibility and capacity:** implementer/operator, approver, validator,
   incident lead, correction actor and verifier; separation, alternates, allocated
   time/funds and escalation for conflict or unavailable review.
5. **Lifecycle decision:** readiness evidence, bounded trial/operation conditions,
   permitted residual risk, authority, expiry and the exact version/class approved.
   State the tested population, conditions, subgroup coverage, acceptance criteria,
   uncertainty and explanation checks; disclose uses outside that evidence.
   Record suspension/restart and retirement decisions separately when they occur.
6. **Human oversight:** concise approval view and access to originals; training,
   safe error-detection exercise, queue load, justified disagreement, skill-retention
   and fallback evidence. Identify what is proposed rather than observed.
7. **Effect and coverage checks:** approved versus delivered target/scope, independent
   delivery source, eligible population/period, matching method, exclusions, missing
   or conflicting records, duplicate or orphan effects and verifier conclusion.
8. **Incident and retirement:** containment, continuing effects, communication,
   pending claims, workaround versus source correction, restart evidence, revoked
   grants/access, lawful disposition and checked transfer of obligations.
9. **Outcome and burden:** separately defined harm/service/access/correction measures,
   numerators/denominators, unknowns, subgroup effects, work/time/cost and comparison
   basis. No invented legitimacy score, ROI or universal cutoff.
10. **Learning/change:** proposed improvement, evidence and dissent, separate
    competent decision, version changed, next observation and retained prior record.

## Status declaration

- Prepared guidance: [which items are recommendations only].
- Real commitments: [actual grants, staff, contracts and controls with evidence].
- Observed operation: [period, cases, source and assessor; or NOT OBSERVED].
- Verified effects: [specific findings and limits; or NOT VERIFIED].
- Open work: [owner, priority, resource/authority dependency and next review].

Keep confidential evidence in the authorized institutional location. A completed
record does not by itself establish legitimate adoption or operating effectiveness.
