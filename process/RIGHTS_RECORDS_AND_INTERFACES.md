# Rights, records, independence and institutional interfaces

**Executive summary:** Protect usable challenges, sensitive evidence and real remedy delivery. Complete these procedures under actual contextual obligations.

[Read the detailed guidance](#detailed-guidance).

## Detailed guidance

**PROPOSED PROCEDURE SET for contextual completion; no compliance certification.**

Use this single supplement when material questions arise, rather than require
several repetitive worksheets for every routine act. It supplies documentary
remediation for gaps in rights, privacy, enforcement and cross-institution work.
Competent legal, affected-person and independence review remains necessary.

## Participation and rights procedure

Identify governing members separately from workers, customers, patients,
beneficiaries and absent/vulnerable people. Record applicable rights, protected
limits and the competent process for disputed objectives. Give intelligible
notice before a materially adverse act where required, expose material reasons,
provide accessible non-AI challenge and allow authorized representation.

For children, impaired capacity or absent beneficiaries, specify who can speak,
what authorization is needed, whose interests may conflict, how their own views
are heard where appropriate and which independent escalation exists. Do not
assume an employee, parent, manager or AI represents everyone legitimately.

A person can challenge without accepting the contested outcome. Specify safe
withdrawal from optional participation, continuing duties and service/claim
continuity; withdrawal from employment, membership and services has different
consequences. Protect confidential reporting and prohibit retaliation in the
actual adopted policy, with a noninvolved route, protective measures and a
competent remedy. A template cannot make those protections effective by itself.

## Records and evidence procedure

Maintain two linked layers: a useful decision/authority record and protected
supporting evidence where required. Use stable identifiers and distinguish
originals from summaries. The decision layer includes material reasons,
uncertainty, alternatives, dissent, authority, effect status and corrections.
Restricted evidence does not mean a reviewer must accept unexplained conclusions.

Before collecting sensitive material, fill this schedule:

| Item | Required contextual decision |
|---|---|
| Purpose and lawful basis | Why each class is needed; actual authority/obligations; avoid unnecessary collection |
| Custody and access | Named custodian, protected reviewer access, authentication and non-AI alternative |
| Public disclosure | Which reasons/aggregates are public; necessary redaction and review of contested restrictions |
| Retention and disposal | Class-specific periods, holds, duties, lawful deletion and preservation of nonpersonal institutional history |
| Correction | Link superseded claim to correction; do not conceal original reliance; distinguish lawful restriction/disposal from falsification |
| AI/provider processing | Permitted inputs, access, retention/cross-use, transfer and incident responsibilities |
| Failure and remedy | Access loss, disclosure error, disputed redaction and correction/notification responsibilities |

Do not upload real confidential evidence to a public repository. No invented
universal retention period is appropriate. v0.1's permanent/public memory and
privacy obligations may conflict in a particular setting; identify the exact
clauses and obtain explicit competent interpretation or change, rather than
pretend a protected annex automatically resolves the conflict.

## Independence and effective-control review

For each decisive source, selector, reviewer, executor and verifier, identify who
controls funding, evidence, models, communications, identity/access, agenda and
replacement. Record common control and the practical ability to disagree,
obtain originals and enforce a remedy. Different prompts or departments may
share the same decisive dependency. Test a shared-sponsor conflict, custodian
refusal, missing alternate and a reviewer who disagrees with an executive.

Under v0.1, IV.2's definition is controlling; this guidance cannot relax it.
In a separate modular adaptation, disclose its independence standard and
limitations explicitly. External review capacity can help but does not become
independent merely by contract. Identify escalation when material independence
cannot be supplied; narrow reliance instead of relabeling the conflict.

## Remedy and resource procedure

A judgment names the actual actor able to implement each part, its permission,
resources, deadline, evidence of delivery, noninvolved verification and escalation
for refusal. Record pending, attempted, completed, failed and disputed statuses
separately. Keep the case open as needed when funds, notice or restoration remain
undelivered. Confirm through original transaction or affected-person evidence,
not the contested AI's completion statement.

Prepare a workload/resource estimate with case volume, preparation/checking
minutes, peak load, appeals, relief coverage and ordinary operating capacity.
State which commitments are real and which are hypothetical. Specify actual
funding-withdrawal and reviewer-removal responses; identifying a budget does not
prove protection against a powerful sponsor. External enforcement powers must
come from real competent institutions.

## Cross-institution interface procedure

For a vendor, partner, authority or joint workflow, record each institution's
actual grant, controlling obligations, affected people, decision/appeal rights,
evidence custodian, disclosure limits and remedy responsibility. Identify
competing directives, unresolved jurisdiction and lawful continuation/stop
options. Do not infer extraterritorial Court jurisdiction or binding power over
a counterparty from an Alvorada-labelled record.

When directives conflict: preserve the conflict, stop only the effect that must
not proceed under the actual rules, use competent escalation, maintain safe
permitted service and track obligations. A commercial sponsor cannot waive a
partner's independent duties. Test refusal of correction and a partner outage.
