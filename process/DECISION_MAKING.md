# Decision-making workflow

**Executive summary:** Identify who may decide, compare permissible options, check evidence and verify what happens. Use only the review depth the actual decision needs.

[Read the detailed guidance](#detailed-guidance).

## Detailed guidance

**PROPOSED SUPPORTING PRACTICE — USE WITH ACTUAL INSTITUTIONAL AUTHORITY**

Alvorada helps people make, explain, challenge and revisit consequential
institutional decisions. Use this entry point with the [blank decision record](templates/DECISION_RECORD.md).
It brings together the existing reviews rather than requiring a separate form
for each research influence. The [business and econometrics review](../research/decision-making/README.md)
records the sources, inclusion choices and evidence limits behind this extension.

For a context-sensitive architecture, choose the [modular charter/profile](../constitutional-design/modular/README.md)
and complete the [profile schedule](templates/INSTITUTIONAL_PROFILE.md).
Use the [complete invented packet](../docs/examples/SERVICE_CASE.md) as a reading
example; it is not an executed case. Other review worksheets are optional
supplements selected for material questions, not a checklist for every reply.

## Choose a proportionate level of review

Use [T0–T4 proportional governance](PROPORTIONAL_GOVERNANCE.md) to assess
consequences, evidence sufficiency, reliance and review burden.
For a bounded routine choice under a clear standing grant, record the actual
authority, relevant evidence, choice, responsible person and review route.
Use a deeper review when consequences, irreversibility, uncertainty, conflicting
interests or disputed scope make that insufficient. Keep links to existing
records rather than copying sensitive evidence into every worksheet.

A longer form does not establish better judgment. Assess whether the added work
helps someone reconstruct, challenge or correct the decision. Follow actual
institutional rules where they require more; this guide cannot waive them.

Use [review calibration](REVIEW_CALIBRATION.md) to specify authorized routes,
timing evidence, response targets and competent alternatives before an incident.
The [record-value study](../testing/programs/RECORD_VALUE.md) tests whether fuller
optional elaboration improves substantive correction enough to justify its burden.

## 1. Define the choice and decision rights

State the action being considered, deadline, affected people and institutional
purpose. Identify the competent human decision-maker and actual grant source,
scope, exclusions and expiry. Distinguish proposer, analyst, decision-maker,
executor, reviewer and verifier; disclose shared people and dependencies.

An option with unresolved authority is not made permissible by its estimated
benefit. Obtain competent clarification or narrow/defer that act through the
actual process. Preserve ordinary authorized service and pending obligations.
The proposed Constitution's membership and standing cannot be narrowed to the
people invited to this decision meeting.

## 2. Make objectives and constraints explicit

Describe the intended benefit and how it will be observed. Separate binding
constraints from preferences that may be traded off. Record affected-person
perspectives, contested priorities and what the chosen metric omits.

If weights, valuations or thresholds matter, identify who selected them and
why. Do not use a weighted benefit score to compensate for a prohibited act,
missing authority or infringement of a binding constraint. Disputed ends may
need a different human decision process before this choice can proceed.

## 3. Develop available alternatives

Compare the proposed action with a credible alternative, narrower or reversible
action, and non-action or deferral where feasible. Explain unavailable options.
Record resource and capacity limits, effects on each affected group, harms of
delay, reversibility and residual obligations. Avoid presenting an implausible
opponent solely to make the preferred option win.

## 4. Separate evidence, predictions and values

For each material claim, link its original evidence, date, applicability and
source dependencies. Mark observation, inference, model prediction, preference
and UNKNOWN separately. Related reports may repeat a single origin.

State important assumptions and a plausible competing account. Describe what
would change the recommendation. For defensible quantitative forecasts, record
the target, horizon, units, basis and uncertainty; otherwise use bounded
scenarios or explicit unknowns. Explore whether a plausible change in an
assumption changes the choice. Numerical precision must not hide weak evidence.

Ask what additional information could change the decision, whether it can be
obtained in time, and whether its burden is proportionate. A test is one possible
learning route, not a default entitlement. It needs its own appropriate scope,
design and actual authority. Use the [evaluation plan](../testing/programs/DECISION_EVALUATION.md)
before attributing effects to an intervention.

## 5. Challenge the recommendation

Where useful, obtain initial judgments before collective discussion; retain
material changes and reasons. Seek the strongest objection, contrary evidence
and affected-person account without inventing testimony. Record conflicts and
who controls the evidence, option list, agenda, resources and review access.

Use the [uncertainty review](DECISION_REVIEW_UNDER_UNCERTAINTY.md) for escalation,
harm and alternative explanations; the [evidence correction ledger](EVIDENCE_CHALLENGE_AND_CORRECTION.md)
for contested claims; and the [cross-disciplinary review](CROSS_DISCIPLINARY_REVIEW.md)
for usable participation and reviewer capacity. Fill the relevant portions and
link them. These are supporting questions, not substitute adjudication.

## 6. Record the accountable decision

The competent human selects a permissible option or records narrowing,
deferral or refusal. Preserve reasons, material dissent, unresolved uncertainty,
actual scope and expiry. An adviser or model can recommend; the recommendation
does not itself authorize an effect.

Set observation and reconsideration triggers, the actual reviewer and a lawful
continuity route. Urgency does not create a general exception. Where the
proposed Article V is relevant, distinguish prior delegation from its narrow
necessity fallback and retain the applicable filing, expiry and review limits.

## 7. Observe execution and effects

Record what was attempted, what actually occurred, unexpected harm, workload
and unfinished obligations. Keep the executor's assertion distinct from
verification evidence. Use existing competent verification routes; do not
appoint constitutional officers by filling a template.

## 8. Review, correct and learn

Review reasoning against information available at decision time, then assess
observed effects separately. Ask whether new evidence warrants correction,
whether a remedy was delivered, and whether the review route worked in practice.
Preserve the old record and link its superseding decision. Identify who owns
remaining obligations through personnel change, transition or termination.

A successful outcome does not expand authority. Repeated operational changes
may raise structural-change or refounding questions under VII; they cannot be
approved through ordinary performance review alone.

## Example: supplier renewal

**Invented illustration — no observed performance or grant.** A purchasing
manager recommends renewing a contract because the supplier's average response
time improved. The reviewer identifies an authorized renewal ceiling, compares
a shorter renewal and a qualified alternative, and notices that failed requests
were excluded from the supplier's metric. Evidence is retained and the missing
denominator is marked UNKNOWN. A competent procurement owner decides whether
to obtain comparable records, narrow the renewal or defer it. That person's
grant must come from the real organization. Later delivery and any correction
remain separate observations; this example reports neither.

## AI support and recursive improvement

[AI institutional support](../applications/ai-assisted-institutional-governance.md)
can prepare source packets and drafts, surface contradictions and monitor pending
remedies. Bounded routine execution needs a real prior grant. Use
[virtuous recursion](VIRTUOUS_RECURSION.md) to turn verified outcomes into proposed
improvements with distinct approval and stable outcome assessment. Time savings
and improved judgment remain hypotheses; measure total checking and correction
work as well as drafting effort.
