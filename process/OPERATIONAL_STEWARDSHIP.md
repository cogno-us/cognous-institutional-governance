# Keep governance working in daily operations

**Executive summary:** know which workflows you govern, name the people who can
maintain and stop them, review material changes and check what actually happened.
Use existing records wherever possible. Routine authorized work should remain
routine; increase review when consequences, uncertainty or exposure increase.

Start with one material workflow and the [optional stewardship record](templates/WORKFLOW_STEWARDSHIP_RECORD.md).
This guide complements the [decision workflow](DECISION_MAKING.md), not every
individual decision record. [Detailed guidance](#detailed-guidance) follows.

## Detailed guidance

**PROPOSED GUIDANCE AND THEORY.** Actual mandates, contextual obligations,
resources and functioning controls are necessary. This file supplies no grant,
constitutional amendment, legal certification, software or demonstrated saving.
Later learning from four external works is documented in the
[enterprise governance review](../research/ENTERPRISE_GOVERNANCE_REVIEW.md).
The organizing choices here are Alvorada's synthesis, not an imported standard.

## 1. Discover and register material workflows

Govern the whole route from purpose and inputs to decisions, actions, affected
people and correction. Include human-only procedures, ordinary automation and
AI-supported work. For AI, consider embedded supplier features and unofficial
employee use, not just models developed internally. Discovery must respect
privacy and employment obligations; it is not permission for covert surveillance.

Use a stable workflow identifier and link existing source records. Record purpose,
operating owner and alternate, affected people, current stage, dependencies,
decision rights, review tier, permitted activity and last meaningful review.
Where material, include versions of policies, models, instructions, sources,
tools and integrations, together with jurisdictions and data restrictions.
Do not publish sensitive inventories in this public repository.

Name the owner of discovery and update, evidence of coverage and unresolved
blind spots. Reconcile known procurement, service, approval and incident records
where authorized. An empty register or a confident AI search does not establish
that nothing is operating outside it. T0 work can share a concise class entry;
it need not generate a separate register entry per response.

## 2. Review dependencies and cumulative exposure

Identify common providers, policies, reviewers, evidence custodians, funding and
fallback resources. Ask whether several individually bounded workflows can harm
the same population, compete for the same reviewer or fail together. Report
shared dependencies alongside individual classifications. Do not average a
severe subgroup harm into a reassuring portfolio score.

For material shared dependence, assess an outage, silent policy/provider change,
evidence loss and a correlated mistake. Identify which services must continue,
which must narrow or stop, and how pending claims remain reachable. A backup
using the same provider and credentials may share the same failure. A human
fallback needs actual competence and capacity; its existence on paper is insufficient.
Use the existing [independence/interface procedure](RIGHTS_RECORDS_AND_INTERFACES.md)
for control and remedy questions.

### Set cumulative resource and retry boundaries

For material continuous or delegated work, identify who owns shared allowances
for spending, execution time, tool calls/retries and other scarce resources.
Define the accounting scope and period, warning/stop conditions, reserved essential
service and competent escalation. A sequence of individually permitted acts can
exceed a shared limit. No default numbers are supplied here. Budget exhaustion
cannot authorize extra spending, hidden model downgrade or an emergency waiver.

Check pending transactions and claims before stopping or retrying. Preserve a
reachable human continuation route and actual state; avoid duplicate obligations
when resuming after an interruption. A dashboard is evidence to inspect, not proof
that a technical limit is enforced. Apply the same reasoning to human workloads.

## 3. Assign operating responsibility without concentrating power

For each material workflow, identify who can:

- Own the purpose and service outcome, and maintain the operational record.
- Implement and operate the procedure or technical safeguards.
- Challenge evidence and validate behavior with appropriate separation.
- Approve use, material change and permissible residual risk under a real mandate.
- Contain incidents and deliver corrections.
- Authorize restart or retirement and independently check material delivery.

Record alternates, time, resources, decision limits and escalation. One named
coordinator can prevent diffuse responsibility; that does not make the coordinator
the sole judge, approver, executor and auditor. Combining roles in a small institution
requires disclosure, compensating review and narrower reliance when independence
is unavailable. A committee's advice is not an operational grant.

Ownership must be usable: can the responsible person obtain originals, disagree
with a sponsor, stop a reserved action and fund a remedy? Protected constraints
and external duties cannot be accepted away as ordinary residual risk. Integrate
with existing lawful management rather than invent a parallel organization.

## 4. Map obligations and suppliers before relying on them

Keep a contextual obligation register: controlling instrument/source, version
and verification date, applicable activity/population/jurisdiction, competent
reviewer, operational duty, responsible owner, evidence and update trigger.
Separate binding obligations, voluntary commitments and internal design choices.
No universal legal checklist or deadline is supplied here; use current primary
sources and qualified review for actual legal conclusions.

For material suppliers, address the institution's responsibilities and the
supplier's actual commitments: approved uses and data handling, subcontractors,
change notification, evidence access/export, incident cooperation, continuity,
exit and disposition. Confirm who can disable newly activated features and what
restrictions are technically possible. A vendor label of "advisory" or "low risk"
does not determine the consequences in the deployed workflow. Certification does
not settle all local obligations. Unavailable critical evidence or controls should
lead to narrower use or an unresolved readiness finding, not assumed assurance.

For assurance reports or certificates, record the issuing body, assessed entity,
product/service, versions, operating region, period, criteria, exclusions and expiry
or change triggers. Compare that scope with this deployment and its evidence needs.
A corporate certificate, contractual promise and verified local configuration are
different evidence. Check shared upstream providers, model substitutions and
subcontractors; several vendor names may conceal one dependency. An outside
reviewer still requires an effective-control/conflict assessment.

Rights requests need an actual route through sources, derived records, retrieval,
outputs, recipients and delegated services. Link the [rights execution procedure](RIGHTS_RECORDS_AND_INTERFACES.md#execute-rights-requests-through-the-workflow),
including verified propagation, lawful preservation and honest technical limits.
A front-end confirmation is not proof that downstream reliance changed.

Use the [rights/records/interface schedule](RIGHTS_RECORDS_AND_INTERFACES.md)
for privacy, custody, retention and counterparties; link it rather than copy it.

## 5. Review the lifecycle and material changes

Treat these as checkpoints under existing authority. Combine checkpoints for
simple work when evidence and grants allow; they are not mandatory new committees.

| Stage | Question before proceeding | Minimum useful record |
|---|---|---|
| Intake | Is the purpose legitimate, who is affected, and is this method suitable? | Owner, scope, alternatives including no AI/no change, initial consequence assessment |
| Preparation | Can the institution operate the safeguards and correction route? | Actual grants, dependencies, constraints, required evidence and acceptance questions |
| Bounded trial | What limits make an authorized trial acceptable? | Scope, participants/protections, stop conditions, baseline and unresolved findings |
| Start/expand | Are conditions met for this particular use? | Competent decision, evidence, residual uncertainties, expiry/review trigger, support and tested fallback |
| Continued use/change | Does current operation remain within the approved basis? | Outcomes, incidents, exceptions, changes, incomplete evidence and renewed decision where needed |
| Suspension/restart | What must stop, what must continue and who may resume? | Containment status, preserved claims, verified correction, restart conditions and responsible approval |
| Retirement | Are active powers removed without abandoning people or evidence? | Permissions/features disabled, pending obligations transferred, lawful data disposition, retained history and delivery check |

Reassess after material changes in purpose, policy, data/source, provider/model,
instructions, connected tools, privileges, delegation, persistence, population,
jurisdiction, volume, human oversight or evidence availability. Include incidents,
near misses and unexpected outcomes. Routine edits that do not change the approved
basis need a proportionate record, not full reapproval. Define the boundary in advance.

Approval attaches to the specified action or bounded class, conditions and duration.
A changed target, amount, recipient or policy can invalidate reliance on an earlier
approval. Automatic vendor updates are still changes. Retirement includes checking
that queues, scheduled jobs, delegated accounts or human procedures do not continue
the withdrawn activity. Preserve pending remedies during replacement.

### Review substitutions and deployment transitions

Specify any permitted model/provider routing and substitutions: purposes,
populations/languages, tool behavior, data restrictions, evidence requirements
and limits. A switch within tested and authorized conditions can remain routine;
a switch beyond that envelope needs the applicable revalidation and decision.
Low price or strong aggregate performance does not establish local suitability.
Record the actual version used in a material case, including fallbacks.

Internal use, staff-facing drafts and externally delivered decisions differ in
population, reliance, consent/access arrangements and possible effects. An internal
trial does not authorize a public launch. Before expansion, review those changes,
reviewer capacity, counterparties, accessibility and remedy/continuity routes.
Employee participation and protections also need actual contextual arrangements;
internal does not automatically mean harmless. Compare simpler automation and
human alternatives when agentic behavior adds little value.

## 6. Keep three assessments separate

| Assessment | What it answers | What it cannot establish |
|---|---|---|
| Consequence/review tier | How severe, irreversible, uncertain or widespread could the effect be? | Technical capability or legal classification |
| Capability and exposure | Is the workflow advisory, acting, connected, persistent or delegating; at what scale? | That a draft-only use is harmless or that autonomy is legitimately granted |
| Assurance readiness | Can people operate, inspect, challenge, contain and correct this particular use? | Authority, consent or acceptable purpose merely from a maturity label |

Apply [T0–T4 proportional governance](PROPORTIONAL_GOVERNANCE.md) to the act.
An AI can suggest classification and flag a change; a competent human owns
material reclassification and actual grants. Do not equate these tiers with a
supplier's autonomy levels, an external maturity ladder or statutory risk classes.
Higher adoption does not imply higher readiness. Uncertain severe effects warrant
investigation; LLM confidence is not calibrated risk or permission.

## 7. Check human review and preserve practical competence

Give reviewers a brief account of the proposed action, material consequences,
authority, original sources, uncertainty and alternatives, with deeper evidence
available. Do not demand raw-log reading for every low-tier act. Ensure enough
time, relevant competence, accessible tools and power to withhold approval.

Review whether humans detect material mistakes, modify proposals, escalate and
reach evidence when needed. Use authorized safe exercises and independently
selected samples. Record response time, queue load and override patterns as
diagnostic signals, not verdicts: low override may mean good proposals, poor
oversight or suppressed disagreement. A justified dissenter is not a defect to
train away. Approval analytics require proportionate data use and fair treatment.

Inform users when AI materially assists a service and explain its scope,
limitations, data use and human challenge route in accessible language. Do not
transfer institutional accountability to customers through a generic disclaimer.
Train staff to recognize changed policies, missing evidence and unavailable
authority. Demonstrate continued manual or alternative operation for critical
tasks; course attendance alone does not prove readiness or retained skill.

### Test suitability and explanations for the intended use

Define what an evaluation can support: purpose, population and relevant subgroups,
site, language, time, input conditions, version, human support and prohibited uses.
Examine whether labels or historical practice embed the harm being evaluated.
Choose and justify acceptance criteria before reviewing results; do not import a
universal fairness ratio or assume aggregate accuracy covers small groups. Report
sample limitations and uncertainty, including groups not adequately observed.
A changed population or operating condition may require narrower reliance and
renewed review. Rights and protected constraints are not averaged away.

Provide reasons appropriate to affected people and reviewers. Separately assess
whether an explanation is intelligible, supported by the actual evidence and
faithful to the relevant decision process. A plausible AI narrative, agreement
between explanation methods or a polished model card proves none of these.
Where reasons cannot be substantiated, preserve that uncertainty and apply the
contextual challenge/containment route rather than manufacture an explanation.
The same questions apply to human scoring rules and ordinary software.

### Check language, intent and accessible action

Assess materially equivalent requests in each intended language, including mixed
language or voice where relevant. Use competent domain/language assessment of
intent, policy/source meaning, tool parameters, substantive disposition and
accessible challenge. Literal translation similarity or fluency alone is insufficient.
Differences can be justified by actual context; preserve that rationale rather
than demand mechanically identical outcomes. Report untested language groups and
language-dependent cost/delay; do not quietly downgrade their safeguards.

An ambiguous request to resolve a problem is not blanket permission to choose
any means. Clarify material ambiguity or obtain a reserved decision while allowing
clearly authorized routine work. Avoid unnecessary questions when the grant and
relevant intent are already clear.

## 8. Verify effects and evidence coverage

For a material act, distinguish the decision/approval record, the authoritative
record of delivery and the reviewer's conclusion. This builds on
[evidence challenge and correction](EVIDENCE_CHALLENGE_AND_CORRECTION.md) and
the [rights/records procedure](RIGHTS_RECORDS_AND_INTERFACES.md).

Check whether the actual recipient, target, scope and timing matched the approved
act and whether the intended effect occurred. Consider a request accepted by a
tool but not committed, partial delivery, a retry producing duplicates, a changed
approval and a transaction without a corresponding decision. Human paperwork can
have equivalent faults. Generated explanations are claims requiring verification,
not faithful access to an AI's internal reasoning.

Per-case completeness and population coverage are different. Define the eligible
case/transaction population, period, authoritative sources, matching method and
known exclusions. Report missing, contradictory, late and unmatched records as
states; do not quietly exclude them from a successful sample. A hash protects a
captured record's integrity under stated assumptions; it proves neither capture
completeness nor substantive truth. Technical delivery does not prove consent,
fairness or lawful authority.

Missing critical evidence triggers a documented uncertainty and containment
decision appropriate to consequences. For severe effects, unavailable evidence
may prevent further reserved action until competent review. Stopping everything
can itself cause harm; use authorized safe degradation and the
[review-calibration guide](REVIEW_CALIBRATION.md), without creating an emergency
waiver. Actual monitoring, permissions and reconciliation need separate engineering.

### Inspect paths, handoffs and composition

For consequential workflows, sample material intermediate actions as well as
final delivery. Check whether sources were fabricated, restrictions changed or
a prohibited act attempted along the path. An acceptable outcome does not erase
a path violation. Preserve blocked attempts separately from delivered effects.

At each material human/agent/partner handoff, preserve task intent, actual grant,
source/version, restrictions, approval conditions, uncertainty, effect status and
responsible counterpart. A recipient must not interpret advice as binding approval.
Review combined behavior and exposure; individual component success does not
establish system success. Trace actual effects through partial delivery and restart,
using authorized records without demanding disclosure of private model cognition.

### Keep approval valid until the effect occurs

Use the [authorization-to-effect contract](AUTHORIZATION_EFFECT_CONTRACT.md) to
bind the approved act, declare decision-critical evidence obligations and recheck
relevant grant/target/policy/currentness conditions at the actual effect boundary.
Distinguish optional UNKNOWN, required-condition UNKNOWN and unconfirmed delivery.
Maintain stable effect identity and separate attempts; reconcile partial or unknown
state before retry. Disclose any check-to-commit gap that the implementation cannot
close. Observers supply evidence and cannot acquire execution power during outages.

## 9. Handle incidents, near misses and restart

Identify a reachable incident lead and alternate, preauthorized containment
options, evidence custodian, affected-service continuity, communication owner
and competent restart decision. Distinguish operational incident handling from
constitutional emergency authority. A technical stop neither undoes a delivered
effect nor disposes of a person's claim.

Record detection, scope, containment, continuing exposure, notifications required
by actual obligations, remedial delivery and unresolved consequences. Preserve
near misses and blocked attempts with proportionate evidence. A high blocked-action
count can reflect good protection or recurring bad proposals; report both.
Separate an interim workaround from correction of the originating cause and
test the affected path before restart. Give material dissent a route beyond the
original operator. Do not let an AI certify its own correction as complete.

### Join security response and institutional accountability

Use a linked case when an incident crosses technical security, service delivery
and rights. Share authorized evidence, a chronology, versions and status rather
than competing accounts. Name who decides containment, institutional consequences,
affected-person communication, remedy and restart under each actual mandate.
A suspicious technical signal does not itself authorize dismissal, benefit denial
or indefinite loss of access. Preserve an accessible challenge and safe service
where required while uncertainty is investigated. Different notification duties
may apply; competent review determines them, not an invented universal deadline.

Coordination does not collapse separation of functions: operators contain, competent
actors decide material consequences, and noninvolved reviewers check correction.
Verify both technical recovery and restoration of affected people's position.
An incident closed in the security queue can remain open as a governance claim.

## 10. Report substantive outcomes and learn without self-authorization

Choose a small set of decision-relevant measures with definitions, owners,
sources, periods, numerators/denominators, missing states and action triggers.
Use the existing [institutional measures](../testing/programs/INSTITUTIONAL_MEASURES.md)
and [causal-evaluation guidance](../research/decision-making/CAUSAL_EVALUATION.md).
Report separately:

- Unauthorized or harmful effects, affected subgroups and verified remedy delivery.
- Legitimate service retained, delay, access to challenge and unresolved complaints.
- Evidence gaps, shared dependencies, incidents and expired/contested exceptions.
- Total preparation, checking, correction, training and infrastructure burden.

Report known commitments separately from observed operation and verified effects.
An executive or board packet should state decisions needed, unresolved exposure,
the responsible actor, response date and consequence of inaction. Preserve
affected-person concerns and how they influenced the response; consultation count
does not establish influence. Do not reduce harm, rights or legitimacy to ROI or
a single maturity score. Assess costs locally rather than import return multipliers.

AI can find stale entries, prepare summaries, flag mismatches and suggest changes.
Humans review material conclusions and independently check source/effect samples.
Follow [virtuous recursion](VIRTUOUS_RECURSION.md): observed problems inform a
proposed improvement; a separate authorized decision changes the process; a new
observation checks effects and burden. Retain failures and prior versions. Learning
cannot authorize the assistant to expand its powers or lower safeguards.

## Adoption and evidence boundary

Start with one bounded workflow, link real grants and existing records, assign
owners and assess the most consequential unresolved dependencies. Expand only
when actual capability supports it. Use the [adoption assessment](../docs/ADOPTION_ASSESSMENT.md)
for commitments and the [proposed stewardship review](../testing/research/OPERATIONAL_STEWARDSHIP_REVIEW.md)
for tabletop questions. Staffing, legal applicability, procurement rights,
technical enforcement, independent review and outcome benefits remain unverified
until tested in the institution. No office size, term or fixed constitutional
threshold changes through this guide.
