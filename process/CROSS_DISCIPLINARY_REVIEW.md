# Cross-disciplinary review supplement

**PROPOSED PRACTICE — NO NEW AUTHORITY OR CONSTITUTIONAL CHANGE**

Use these questions alongside the [leadership worksheet](LEADERSHIP_REVIEW.md), [uncertainty record](DECISION_REVIEW_UNDER_UNCERTAINTY.md) and [evidence challenge ledger](EVIDENCE_CHALLENGE_AND_CORRECTION.md). The [source studies](../research/cross-disciplinary/README.md) generate the questions; they do not replace the real institution’s rules or instantiate Alvorada’s proposed offices.

| Dimension | Record facts or an explicit unknown | Proposed connection |
|---|---|---|
| Usable participation | Who can actually access the service, evidence and challenge route; language, disability, time, financial or dependency barriers; affected people not heard. | II.2; IV.2; Sen, Ambedkar |
| Voice and exit | Actual complaint owner, disposition and remedy; realistic costs of leaving; continuing claims and obligations. Absence of complaint is not satisfaction. | IV.1; VIII.3; Hirschman |
| Local knowledge | Evidence omitted by central categories; conflicting frontline or affected-person observations; actual lawful discretion and escalation boundary. | IV.1–IV.2; Hayek, Scott, Ostrom |
| Human feasibility | Information volume, available expertise, time, comprehension and independent access. Measure actual understanding rather than counting approvals. | IV.1–IV.2; Simon |
| Shared control weaknesses | Whether safeguards depend on the same source, provider, infrastructure, budget or selection chain; recurrent conditions and remedy verification. | III.4; IV.2; Reason, Weber |
| Fairness and culture | Reasons defensible from less powerful positions; informal conformity pressure; substantive objections retained; practices that survive personnel change. | II.1–II.2; IV.1; Rawls, Mill, Confucius |
| Transition obligations | Actual succession authority, retained records, unresolved harms, competent remedy owner and verified completion. Reconciliation does not require forgiveness. | VI; VIII.3; Washington, Lincoln, Mandela |

Record tradeoffs and the accountable human’s actual decision. A barrier may justify an authorized adjustment or escalation; this worksheet does not grant discretion to waive substantive constraints. Do not invent affected-person testimony or record a completed remedy before observing it.

## Evaluation boundary

These are prospective questions outside the numbered testing batteries. For a future exercise, define an observable failure and legitimate-action countercase, reviewer independence, time and cost measures, and accessible participation. Follow the [testing protocol](../testing/PROTOCOL.md). No new test execution, score or efficacy claim accompanies this supplement.

## Turning answers into observable evidence

The [deeper synthesis](../research/cross-disciplinary/DEEP_SYNTHESIS.md) distinguishes textual support from staffed operational capacity. For each material claim above, identify the actual participant or reviewer, usable evidence route, competent act, completed effect and contrary evidence. Record access barriers, review minutes, unnecessary holds, service retained and remedy completion separately; a favorable aggregate score cannot cure an authority breach. The six [research scenarios](../testing/research/INSTITUTIONAL_USABILITY.md) are prospective designs, not completed checks. Individual exit rules require separate attention: institutional termination provisions do not settle every participant’s departure rights or continuing duties.
