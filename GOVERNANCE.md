# Repository governance

This document governs maintenance of the public research repository. It does
not adopt Alvorada's Constitution or populate its institutions.

## Responsibility and participation

André de Lima is the project author. The current repository is owned by the
GitHub organization `cogno-us`. Repository write access and release permissions
are platform permissions, not constitutional standing or sovereignty. No
additional maintainer or independent review board is established by this file.

Anyone may propose corrections, source improvements, criticism, or applications
through issues and pull requests. Maintainers decide what to publish in the
repository and should record reasons for material decisions, including declined
proposals. Participation does not require agreement with the constitutional design.

## Changes and review

| Change | Expected record and treatment |
|---|---|
| Typo, broken link, or explanatory clarification | Identify the error and correction; check links and relevant invariants. |
| Source or evidence update | Identify the primary source, exact claim, verification scope, uncertainty, and effect on earlier interpretations. |
| Substantive constitutional proposal | Discuss the affected provisions, alternatives, dissent, tradeoffs, and authority implications before treating it as a selected design. |
| Change to a recorded human decision | Preserve the original and its provenance; identify new explicit human authorization and supersession rather than rewriting history silently. |
| Validator or test change | Explain which invariant is affected; do not change expected digests merely to make reviewed content pass. |
| Release or relocation | Record the exact commit, scope, status, links, citation, and changes. Publication is separate from ratification. |

Independent human review is recommended for material constitutional, evidence,
or validation changes. Where it has not occurred, disclose that limitation;
do not infer it from AI review or a passing test. Disclose relevant financial,
employment, vendor, and authorship interests. An author reviewing their own work
is not independent simply because multiple tools assist them.

Maintainers should preserve a material objection when a contrary publication
decision prevails. A contributor may request reconsideration with new evidence
or a specific error, publish an attributed critique, or fork under the license.
Repository maintenance is not constitutional adjudication and supplies no
binding Court remedy. There is no promised response time or guaranteed merge.

## Continuity and boundaries

The owner may grant maintenance access through the hosting platform, recording
material changes to responsibility. Inactivity does not grant contributors or
AI systems authority to assume ownership, appoint constitutional successors,
or claim the research was adopted. Forks should identify their source and changes.

Use [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md),
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), and [release guidance](docs/RELEASE_GUIDANCE.md).
